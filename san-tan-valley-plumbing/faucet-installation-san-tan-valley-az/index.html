    Touchless Faucet Installation in San Tan Valley

    Touchless faucets from Moen, Delta and Kohler use sensors, solenoids and either batteries or a plug-in power supply. We mount the control box under the sink where it stays dry and accessible. We test sensor range and flow so the faucet responds reliably. Hard water can affect solenoids over time, so we add an inline filter when recommended.

    Wall-Mount Faucet Installation in San Tan Valley, AZ

    Wall-mount faucets need a rough-in valve inside the wall, so they are best installed during a remodel. We set the valve at the right height and depth for your sink and finished wall surface.
