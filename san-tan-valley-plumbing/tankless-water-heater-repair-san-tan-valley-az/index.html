    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in San Tan Valley

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, restricting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves and run a descaling solution through the exchanger. Annual descaling is wise for most homes on local water.

    Ignition Failure Repair in San Tan Valley, AZ

    If your tankless unit will not fire, the cause may be a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. We test each component and measure gas pressure at the inlet. Undersized gas lines are a common hidden cause when a tankless unit replaced a tank. We correct the issue so the unit lights reliably.

    Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in San Tan Valley

    Tankless heaters rely on flow sensors to start and thermistors to regulate temperature. A dirty inlet filter or failed sensor causes no hot water, fluctuating temperature or the unit turning off mid-shower. We clean filters, test sensor readings and replace faulty parts. Temperature becomes steady again once readings are accurate.

    Condensing Tankless Repair in San Tan Valley, AZ

    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate. Clogged condensate traps, failed neutralizers and blocked drains can shut the unit down. We clean the trap, replace the neutralizer media and confirm proper drainage. PVC venting is also checked for slope and sagging.

    Recirculation Pump Repair in San Tan Valley

    Many tankless systems use internal or external recirculation to deliver hot water faster. Pump failures, check valve problems or wrong schedule settings can cause long waits or lukewarm water. We test the pump, check valves and controls and set the schedule to match your routine. A properly tuned system saves both water and gas.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in San Tan Valley, AZ

    Electric tankless units from brands like Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart use multiple heating elements and require heavy electrical service. Common problems include burned elements, tripped breakers and failed flow switches. We test elements, voltage and amperage and replace failed components. We also confirm the electrical supply matches the unit's demand.

    Emergency Tankless Repair in San Tan Valley

    A tankless unit that leaks internally or shows a fault you cannot clear needs quick attention. We prioritize no-hot-water calls and arrive with common sensors, igniters and filters. If a major component like a heat exchanger has failed, we check warranty coverage and order the part. We can often restore partial operation in the meantime.

    Tankless Repair vs Replacement Cost in San Tan Valley, AZ

    Descaling service usually costs $175 to $350, and sensor or igniter repairs run $250 to $600. Heat exchanger replacement can cost $800 to $1,500 or more unless covered by warranty. A new tankless unit installed typically ranges from $3,000 to $5,500. We compare these costs against the age and condition of your unit.
