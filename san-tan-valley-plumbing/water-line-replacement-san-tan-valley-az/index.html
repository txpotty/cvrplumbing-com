    Durable Water Line Replacement Services in San Tan Valley, AZ

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in San Tan Valley

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and rusty water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often immediate.

    Polybutylene Water Line Replacement in San Tan Valley, AZ

    Polybutylene pipe was used in some homes built in the 1980s and 1990s. It can become brittle and fail without warning. Many insurers flag it as a risk. We replace polybutylene service lines with durable materials. This reduces the chance of sudden leaks.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in San Tan Valley

    Trenchless methods let us pull new pipe through the ground with minimal digging. This protects driveways, mature trees and landscaping. We use pipe pulling or directional boring depending on conditions. Not every property is a candidate, especially with heavy rock. We explain the best option after inspection.

    Copper Service Line Installation in San Tan Valley, AZ

    Type K copper is a durable option for underground water lines. It resists punctures and lasts for decades. Copper costs more than PEX but is preferred by some homeowners. We install copper with proper bedding to protect it. We can also install sleeves where it passes through foundations.

    PEX Water Line Installation in San Tan Valley

    PEX is flexible, cost effective and resists scale buildup. It is a popular choice for water line replacement. We use PEX rated for direct burial and protect it from UV exposure. It can be pulled in long runs with fewer fittings. Fewer fittings means fewer leak points.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Upgrades in San Tan Valley, AZ

    Many Arizona homes have high street pressure that can stress pipes and fixtures. We install pressure reducing valves set to a safe range. We also replace old gate valves with full-port ball valves at the house. This makes it easier to shut off water in an emergency. These upgrades protect your plumbing system.

    Commercial Water Service Replacement in San Tan Valley

    Commercial properties often have larger service lines and backflow assemblies. We replace commercial water lines and coordinate with the utility when needed. We schedule work to limit disruption to tenants and customers. Backflow devices are tested and certified. We handle permits and inspections.
