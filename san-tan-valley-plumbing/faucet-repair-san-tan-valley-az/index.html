    Lasting Faucet Repair Services in San Tan Valley, AZ

    Dripping Faucet Repair in San Tan Valley

    A dripping spout usually means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight. We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from adding up on your water bill.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in San Tan Valley, AZ

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base points to failed O-rings or a loose mounting nut. If left alone, the leak can soak into the cabinet below and damage the sink base. We replace O-rings, tighten mounting hardware and reseal the base. We also check the deck for water damage.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in San Tan Valley

    Weak or sputtering flow is often caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-home water pressure.

    Kitchen Faucet Repair in San Tan Valley, AZ

    Kitchen faucets get heavy daily use, and pull-down hoses, spray heads and diverters wear out. We repair leaking hoses, replace sticky diverters and fix weak counterweights on Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister models. Touchless faucets may need new solenoids or batteries. We also fix side sprayers that leak or lose pressure.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in San Tan Valley

    Bathroom sink faucets develop drips, loose handles and broken pop-up drains. We replace cartridges and stems, tighten loose spouts and repair or replace pop-up assemblies. For widespread and centerset faucets, we match parts to the brand and finish. A repaired bathroom faucet works smoothly and looks like new.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in San Tan Valley, AZ

    If water keeps running from the tub spout while the shower is on, the diverter is worn. We replace diverter spouts, stems and gates and repair tub faucet drips. Slip-on and threaded spouts each install differently, so we bring the right type for your setup. This repair restores full shower pressure.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in San Tan Valley

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from intense sun and, in cooler parts of the state, the occasional hard freeze. We repair leaking packing nuts, replace worn washers and install new frost-free hose bibs when needed. We also add vacuum breakers to meet backflow prevention requirements. A leaking hose bib can waste a lot of water over a long, hot summer.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in San Tan Valley, AZ

    A cartridge repair often costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life. However, if the faucet body is cracked, parts are discontinued or it has needed several repairs, replacement may be better value. We give you both prices so you can choose. Many brands like Moen and Delta offer lifetime warranties on cartridges, which can lower your repair cost.
