    Professional Urinal Installation Services in Marana, AZ

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Marana

    Manual flushometer urinals are the standard in many commercial restrooms. We install Sloan Royal or Zurn flush valves with the correct gallons-per-flush rating, typically 0.125 to 0.5 gpf. Each valve includes a vacuum breaker and stop. We adjust the flush to clear the bowl without wasting water.

    Sensor Urinal Installation in Marana, AZ

    Touchless sensor flush valves improve hygiene and reduce wasted flushes. We install battery or hardwired sensor valves and set the detection range for each fixture. Hardwired units may need an electrician for the power supply, which we can coordinate. Sensor valves are common in schools, gyms and healthcare offices.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Marana

    Waterless urinals use a sealed cartridge or trap liquid instead of a flush. They save thousands of gallons per fixture each year and suit Arizona's water-conscious businesses. Proper slope and drain material are important to prevent buildup, so we size and set the drain to the manufacturer's specs. We also explain cartridge maintenance to your staff.

    Residential Urinal Installation in Marana, AZ

    Home urinals are increasingly popular in garages, game rooms and busy family bathrooms. We tie into existing drain and vent lines, install a compact flush valve or tank-style system, and finish the wall. This can save water and reduce toilet traffic. We check that your drain line can handle the new fixture.

    ADA Urinal Installation in Marana

    Accessible urinals must have the rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor and enough clear floor space in front. We set the carrier and fixture to meet these rules and place the flush control within reach. This is essential for public restrooms and new construction. We review the layout before setting the fixture.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Marana, AZ

    Older urinals often use 1 gallon or more per flush and have cracked or stained bowls. We remove the old fixture, inspect the waste outlet and carrier, and install a modern low-flow model. Sometimes the old carrier can be reused, which lowers cost. Upgrading also improves restroom appearance and odor control.

    Urinal Rough-In and New Restroom Plumbing in Marana

    For new restrooms or tenant improvements, we rough in the waste, vent and supply lines before walls are closed. We set the outlet height and spacing to the fixture's spec sheet and inspection requirements. Planning ahead avoids costly wall rework later. We coordinate with your contractor on the schedule.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Marana, AZ

    Costs depend on the fixture type, whether a carrier is needed, and how far the drain and supply must run. Sensor valves cost more than manual valves, and waterless urinals have higher upfront prices but save water over time. Replacements using existing lines are the least expensive. We provide a detailed quote that shows each cost.
