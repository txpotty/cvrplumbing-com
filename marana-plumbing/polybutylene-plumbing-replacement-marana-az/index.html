    Lasting Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement Services in Marana, AZ

    Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in Marana

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. You may see it at the water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff. We inspect visible lines, check the stamps and trace runs through the attic. You get a clear answer and a written replacement estimate.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in Marana, AZ

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance. Most homes are completed in two to four days.

    Copper Replacement Option in Marana

    Some homeowners choose Type L copper for its long history and rigid feel. We solder with lead-free solder or use pressed fittings for faster installation. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install. We help you weigh the options for your budget and water conditions.

    Yard Service Line Replacement in Marana, AZ

    Many poly-era homes also have blue or black polybutylene service lines running from the water meter to the house. These buried lines can split and leak under your yard, raising your bill and soaking the soil. We call Arizona 811 to mark utilities, trench the route and install new PEX or copper service pipe. The new line connects to a new main shutoff at the house.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Marana

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. A burst fitting in an attic or wall can cause major damage in minutes. We respond to emergency leaks, make temporary repairs and then schedule a full replacement. Stopping the problem for good is the only lasting fix.

    Manufactured and Mobile Home Poly Replacement in Marana, AZ

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or belly. We replace these lines with PEX sized for manufactured homes and secure them properly underneath. We also check heat tape and insulation on exposed lines in higher elevation areas with winter freezes. The result is a reliable, modern supply system.

    Insurance and Home Sale Documentation in Marana

    Some insurers decline or surcharge policies on homes with polybutylene. Buyers and lenders may also ask about it during inspections. After replacement we provide permit records, invoices and photos showing the new system. That paperwork helps with insurance renewals and real estate transactions.

    Polybutylene Replacement Costs in Marana, AZ

    Replacing polybutylene with PEX in an average Arizona home often costs $5,000 to $12,000, depending on size and bathrooms. Copper systems usually cost more. Adding a yard service line replacement often adds $1,500 to $4,000. Compared to repairing water damage from a burst, a planned replacement is usually the smarter investment.
