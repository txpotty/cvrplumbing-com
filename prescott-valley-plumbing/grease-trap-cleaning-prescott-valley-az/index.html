    Thorough Grease Trap Cleaning Services in Prescott Valley, AZ

    Indoor Grease Trap Cleaning in Prescott Valley

    Small hydromechanical traps under sinks fill quickly in busy kitchens and often need cleaning every few weeks. We remove the lid, pump or hand-scoop the grease and solids, and scrape the baffles. We check the gasket, flow control fitting and inlet for damage. Then we reassemble the unit and confirm water flows through correctly. This keeps odors down and prevents slow sink drainage.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Prescott Valley, AZ

    Large in-ground interceptors require a vacuum truck to fully pump out grease, water and settled solids. We open each compartment, pump it down and use high-pressure water to break up hardened grease on the walls. We inspect inlet and outlet tees, baffles and risers while the tank is empty. Extreme summer heat can make grease odors stronger, so we also check lid seals. A complete pump-out restores full retention capacity.

    Scheduled Grease Trap Maintenance Plans in Prescott Valley

    Many local pretreatment programs expect traps to be cleaned before grease and solids reach a set percentage of capacity, often around 25 percent. We set up recurring cleanings based on your volume and trap size. Our team tracks your schedule and arrives on time without reminders. Consistent maintenance helps you avoid emergency calls and violation notices. Plans can be adjusted as your business changes.

    Emergency Grease Trap Cleaning in Prescott Valley, AZ

    A full grease trap can cause sinks to back up, floor drains to overflow and foul odors to fill the dining room. We respond quickly to emergency calls and can often clear the problem the same day. Our crews pump the trap, clear the lines and check for downstream blockages. If the sewer line is also clogged, we can jet it during the same visit. Fast service helps you get back to serving customers.

    Grease Line Jetting in Prescott Valley

    Grease that escapes the trap often coats the line between the kitchen and the interceptor or the sewer. We use hydro jetting to scour the inside of the pipe and remove hardened grease. Jetting is far more effective than a standard drain snake for grease buildup. We can run a camera afterward to confirm the line is clear. This prevents repeat backups and keeps your trap working properly.

    Compliance Documentation for FOG Programs in Prescott Valley, AZ

    Cities with fats, oils and grease programs often require restaurants to keep cleaning records on site. After every service we provide a record showing the date, volume removed and condition of the unit. These records help you during inspections and can protect you from fines. We can also send copies directly to your manager or corporate office. Good documentation shows you are doing your part to protect the sewer system.

    Odor Control and Trap Inspections in Prescott Valley

    Persistent odors usually point to a trap that is overdue, a missing seal or a damaged baffle. We inspect gaskets, lids and vent connections during every cleaning. If we find damage, we explain the issue and offer repair options. Sealing problems early keeps odors out of your kitchen and dining room. It also prevents grease from bypassing the trap entirely.

    Grease Trap Cleaning Cost in Prescott Valley, AZ

    Cleaning cost depends on trap size, how full it is and how often it is serviced. Small indoor traps generally cost less per visit than large outdoor interceptors that require a vacuum truck. Traps that are rarely cleaned take longer because hardened grease must be broken up. A regular schedule often lowers the cost per visit. We give you clear pricing before work begins.
