    A burst pipe can release several gallons per minute into walls, ceilings or floors. Common causes include high pressure, corrosion, physical damage and the occasional hard freeze.     Sewer Backup Emergency Repair in Prescott Valley, AZ

    When sewage backs up into tubs, showers or floor drains, you have a health hazard and a serious mess. We clear the main line with an auger or hydro-jetter and run a camera to find the cause. Roots, grease, wipes and broken pipes are the usual culprits. We explain repair options if the line is damaged.

    Water Heater Emergency Repair in Prescott Valley

    A leaking or failed water heater can flood a garage or closet and leave you without hot water. We shut off water and fuel, drain the tank and assess whether repair or replacement is best. Our trucks often carry common tank sizes for same-day replacement. We also repair gas valves, thermocouples, elements and relief valves.

    Overflowing Toilet Emergency Repair in Prescott Valley, AZ

    An overflowing toilet can quickly damage flooring and the ceiling below. We clear the clog, repair or replace the fill valve and flapper and check the drain line for deeper blockages. If the toilet is cracked or the wax ring has failed, we replace it. We clean up and sanitize the area.
