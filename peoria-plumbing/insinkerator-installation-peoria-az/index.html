    Badger Series Disposal Installation in Peoria

    The InSinkErator Badger line is the most common replacement disposal in local kitchens. These 1/3 to 3/4 HP units handle everyday scraps and fit most standard cabinets. We install the Badger with its quick-lock mount, attach the discharge tube and connect a cord kit if your old unit was plug-in. It is a budget-friendly choice for rentals, starter homes and light cooking households. Most Badger installs take about an hour.

    Evolution Series Upgrades in Peoria, AZ

    Evolution models like the Compact, Septic Assist and Excel use multi-stage grinding and extra sound insulation. They break down fibrous foods such as celery and corn husks that can jam lighter units. We install Evolution disposals for families who cook often and want a quieter kitchen. Because these units are taller and heavier, we check cabinet clearance and trap height before mounting. The result is a smoother grind and fewer clogs downstream.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Peoria

    If your kitchen has never had a disposal, we add one from scratch. That means installing a new sink flange, adjusting the drain tailpiece, and running power to the cabinet if no outlet exists. We coordinate with a licensed electrician when a new circuit is needed. We also confirm your drain line slope can carry ground waste to the main line. This turns a plain sink into a fully functional prep station.

    Disposal Replacement and Haul-Away in Peoria, AZ

    When an old disposal hums, leaks from the bottom or trips the reset button constantly, replacement is usually smarter than repair. We remove the failed unit, clean the old putty off the sink and install a new InSinkErator in its place. If your old mount is an InSinkErator quick-lock, we can often reuse it to save time. We haul the old unit away so you are not stuck disposing of it. Most replacements finish in a single visit.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Peoria

    Most InSinkErator units have a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. We knock that plug out, clear the debris and connect the dishwasher drain hose with a proper clamp. We then install or verify an air gap or high loop so dirty sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher. Forgetting the knockout is one of the most common DIY mistakes and leads to a dishwasher that will not drain. We test both appliances together before we leave.

    Septic-Friendly Disposal Installs in Peoria, AZ

    Homes on septic systems, common in rural parts of the state, need a disposal that does not overload the tank. InSinkErator's Septic Assist model injects an enzyme solution to help break down food waste. We install the unit, mount the enzyme cartridge and explain how often to replace it. We also talk through scraping habits that keep solids out of the tank. This protects your drain field and extends time between pump-outs.

    Hard Water and Mineral Considerations in Peoria

    Hard water leaves scale on disposal grind rings, sink flanges and drain tailpieces. Over time that buildup can make a disposal noisy and cause slow drainage. We inspect the drain line for scale and debris before connecting a new unit. If heavy buildup is present, we recommend cleaning or replacing the trap and tailpiece at the same time. Stainless steel grind components on Evolution models resist corrosion better in these conditions.

    InSinkErator Installation Cost Comparison in Peoria, AZ

    A basic Badger 5 install with a cord kit usually runs less than a premium Evolution Excel installation. Labor is similar for most swaps, but first-time installs cost more because of new flange work and electrical runs. We give you an upfront price that includes the unit, labor and haul-away. We also compare horsepower, sound levels and warranty length side by side. That way you can choose the model that fits your budget and cooking habits.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Insinkerator Installation in Peoria

    Most InSinkErator replacements cost between $300 and $800 installed, depending on the model. A Badger 5 sits at the lower end, while Evolution Excel installs land at the higher end. First-time installs that need a new outlet or flange work can run more.

    If your old disposal was an InSinkErator with a quick-lock mount in good condition, we can often reuse it. If the mount is corroded, bent or from another brand, we install a new one for a reliable seal.

    InSinkErator offers in-home warranties that range from about 2 years on basic Badger models up to 10 years on premium Evolution units. We also back our installation labor with our own workmanship guarantee.

    A simple replacement generally does not need a permit. Adding a new electrical circuit or making significant drain changes may require one, and we will let you know if your job falls into that category.

    Many homeowners can swap a disposal, but common mistakes include leaving the dishwasher knockout in place, poor flange seals and unsafe wiring. A professional install avoids leaks, tripped breakers and water damage to the cabinet.

    Constant humming without grinding, leaks from the bottom of the unit, frequent tripping of the reset button and rusty water are all signs of failure. If the unit is more than 10 years old, replacement is usually the better value.

    For light use, a Badger 5 or Badger 5XP works well. Families who cook a lot or want a quieter unit should consider an Evolution Compact or Excel. Homes on septic should look at the Evolution Septic Assist.
