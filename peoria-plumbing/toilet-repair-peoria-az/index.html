    Dependable Toilet Repair Services in Peoria, AZ

    Running Toilet Repair in Peoria

    A toilet that keeps refilling is almost always losing water past the flapper or through a fill valve set too high. Hard water leaves mineral deposits on the flush valve seat, so even a new flapper can leak if the seat is rough. We clean or replace the seat, install a properly sized flapper and adjust the float so water stops below the overflow tube. A running toilet can waste a large amount of water each day, so this is one of the fastest repairs to pay for itself.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Peoria, AZ

    Water around the base usually means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. On slab foundations, the flange often sits on concrete and can crack when bolts are overtightened. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a fresh seal. We also check for soft flooring so you know if any water damage needs attention.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Peoria

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet noticeably quieter.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Peoria, AZ

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem may be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Peoria

    Pressure-assist toilets from Sloan Flushmate and dual-flush models from TOTO and American Standard use parts that hardware stores rarely stock. We carry cartridges, flush buttons and seals for the common models and can order specialty parts quickly. These systems need correct pressure settings to work well, so we test incoming water pressure as part of the repair.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Peoria, AZ

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line during every visit. If the porcelain is cracked we will price a replacement tank or new toilet, and we upgrade old plastic or vinyl supply lines to braided stainless.

    Commercial Toilet Repair in Peoria

    Restaurants, offices and retail spaces depend on working restrooms. We repair flushometer valves from Sloan and Zurn, replace diaphragms and vacuum breakers, and fix wall-hung carriers that have loosened. We can schedule work before opening or after close so customers are not affected.

    Emergency Toilet Repair in Peoria, AZ

    An overflowing toilet or a leak coming through the ceiling below cannot wait. We answer emergency calls quickly and walk you through shutting off the angle stop while we are on the way. Once on site we stop the leak, clear the blockage and make a permanent repair when possible, or a safe temporary one until parts arrive.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Repair in Peoria

    Most simple toilet repairs, like a new flapper or fill valve, run about $125 to $275 including parts and labor. Resetting a toilet on a new wax ring is usually $175 to $350, and flange repairs can bring the total to $300 to $500. We give you a flat price before starting.

    Swapping a flapper is a reasonable DIY job if you match the right size and the flush valve seat is smooth. Base leaks, flange repairs and cracked tanks are better left to a plumber because a bad reset can cause hidden water damage to the subfloor.

    Watch for phantom refills, hissing from the tank, a toilet that rocks, stains or soft flooring at the base and frequent clogs. Catching these early keeps a small repair from turning into a floor replacement.
