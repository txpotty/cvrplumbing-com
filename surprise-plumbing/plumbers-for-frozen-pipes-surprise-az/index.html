    Dependable Frozen Pipe Thawing and Repair in Surprise, AZ

    Frozen Pipe Thawing in Surprise

    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line somewhere. We open a faucet downstream to relieve pressure, then locate the frozen section with thermal imaging. Copper and galvanized pipes can be thawed with an electric pipe thawing machine that warms the metal from the inside. PEX and CPVC are warmed gently with heat guns or infrared heaters to avoid softening the plastic.

    Burst Pipe Repair in Surprise, AZ

    Water expands as it freezes, and that pressure can split copper, crack PVC fittings and pop PEX connections. Leaks usually appear only after the ice melts, sometimes hours later. We shut off the water, open the wall or ceiling only where needed and replace the damaged section. Every repair is pressure tested so you know the line is sound.

    Attic and Exterior Wall Pipe Repair in Surprise

    Many Arizona homes run supply lines through attics to reach bathrooms and kitchens, and attics get as cold as the outside air at night. Pipes in exterior walls with thin insulation are also at risk. We repair freeze-damaged sections and add proper insulation or reroute lines into conditioned space. These changes greatly reduce the odds of a repeat freeze.

    Hose Bib and Outdoor Faucet Repair in Surprise, AZ

    Outdoor faucets freeze easily, especially with a hose left connected. A split often hides inside the wall and only shows when the faucet is turned on in spring. We replace damaged hose bibs with frost-free models from brands like Woodford and Prier that shut off water inside the warm part of the wall. Insulated covers add more protection on cold nights.

    Backflow Preventer and Irrigation Freeze Repair in Surprise

    Backflow preventers and irrigation valves sit above ground and are exposed to the coldest air. Frozen backflow assemblies can crack their bodies or bonnets, causing leaks and failed tests. We repair or replace the assembly, insulate it with a fitted cover and recommend draining when deep cold is forecast. Many water providers require a passing backflow test after repairs, and we can arrange that.

    Pool, Cooler and Water Heater Line Freeze Damage in Surprise, AZ

    Pool equipment lines, evaporative cooler supply tubing and water heater connections in garages are all common freeze points in Arizona. Thin copper or plastic tubing feeding roof coolers splits easily in a hard freeze. We repair these lines and can add shutoff and drain valves so they can be winterized quickly. If a water heater connection or valve was damaged, we repair it and check the unit.

    Emergency Frozen Pipe Service in Surprise

    If water is spraying or pouring from a ceiling, shut off the main water valve and call us right away. We respond quickly to active leaks during cold snaps and stop the water first. Once the leak is controlled, we thaw any remaining frozen sections and restore service. We can also document the damage for your insurance claim.

    Frozen Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Surprise, AZ

    Thawing an accessible frozen pipe usually costs $150 to $400. Repairing a burst pipe typically costs $250 to $800 for an accessible line and more when walls or ceilings must be opened. Replacing a hose bib with a frost-free model runs about $200 to $450. Prevention work like insulation and heat cable usually costs far less than repairing a single burst line and its water damage.
