    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Casas Adobes

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Casas Adobes, AZ

    Air switches let you start the disposal with a button on the sink or countertop. This avoids drilling for a new wall switch. We install the button and control box under the sink. Air switches are popular in kitchen remodels and islands.

    When a dishwasher drains through the disposal, the knockout plug must be removed and the drain hose connected. We install an air gap or high loop to prevent backflow. This keeps dirty water from flowing back into the dishwasher. We test both appliances together.
