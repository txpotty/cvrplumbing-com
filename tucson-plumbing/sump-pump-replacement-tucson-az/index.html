    Heavy-Duty Sump Pump Replacement Services in Tucson, AZ

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Tucson

    Submersible sump pumps sit at the bottom of the pit and are quieter and more powerful than pedestal models. We replace failed units with cast iron pumps like the Zoeller M53 or Liberty 250 series. Cast iron dissipates heat better than plastic, which helps the motor last. We check pit size to make sure the float has room to move freely. Most replacements take one to two hours.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Tucson, AZ

    Pedestal pumps have the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and good for narrow pits. They are louder but often last longer since the motor stays dry. We replace worn pedestal pumps and adjust float rods for proper cycling. If your pit is large enough, we may suggest upgrading to a submersible. We explain the trade-offs clearly.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Tucson

    Many sump pump failures start with a stuck float switch or a leaking check valve. A failed check valve lets water flow back into the pit and causes the pump to short cycle. We install new check valves with every pump replacement and verify the switch moves freely. Vertical float switches work well in tight pits. Tethered floats suit larger pits.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Tucson, AZ

    Monsoon storms can knock out power at the exact moment your pump needs to run. A battery backup pump takes over when the main pump fails or loses power. We install backup systems with sealed batteries and alarms. Water-powered backups are another option where water pressure is adequate. We help you pick the right backup for your home.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Tucson

    Basement bathrooms and laundry rooms below the sewer line need sewage ejector pumps instead of sump pumps. These pumps handle solids and must be installed in sealed, vented basins. We replace ejector pumps with models rated for two inch solids. We also check the basin seal, vent and discharge check valve. Proper installation prevents sewer gas odors.

    Yard Drainage Pit Pumps in Tucson, AZ

    Some Arizona properties use pumps in yard drainage pits or below-grade window wells to handle monsoon runoff. These pumps may sit idle for months, then fail when they are needed most. We replace them with corrosion-resistant models and clear debris from the pit. We can route discharge away from foundations and onto approved areas. Regular testing before monsoon season is a good habit.

    Commercial Pit Pump Replacement in Tucson

    Commercial buildings often have elevator pits, parking garage drains and mechanical room sumps. These require pumps rated for higher flow and sometimes oil-sensing controls. We replace commercial pumps and alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. Duplex systems with alternating pumps provide redundancy. We schedule work to avoid disrupting operations.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Tucson, AZ

    If your pit is filling and the pump will not run, call (928) 470-9009 right away. We offer emergency sump pump replacement to stop water before it reaches your floor. Keep the pit lid off and use a wet vac if water is rising. We carry common pumps so we can often replace on the spot. We also check why the old pump failed.
