    Backflow Testing and Repair in Tucson

    Most Arizona water providers require annual testing of backflow prevention assemblies on commercial properties. We test reduced pressure zone, double check and pressure vacuum breaker assemblies with calibrated gauges. When a device fails, we rebuild it with manufacturer kits from Watts, Wilkins or Febco. We submit the results to your water provider so you stay in compliance.

    Grease Trap and Interceptor Service in Tucson, AZ

    Restaurants and food service sites must keep grease out of the public sewer. We inspect and service grease traps and interceptors, replace baffles and clear the lines leading to them. Hydro jetting removes hardened grease from kitchen drains that cables cannot clear. Regular service helps you pass health inspections and avoid sewer backups during dinner rush.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Tucson

    Busy restrooms take a beating, and a broken flushometer or leaking urinal frustrates customers. We repair and replace Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers. We also install ADA-compliant fixtures and grab bar backing during upgrades. Quick repairs keep your facilities clean and usable.

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Tucson, AZ

    Commercial kitchens, gyms and multi-family buildings need large amounts of hot water. We service and install high-input tank water heaters, tankless racks and boiler-fed systems with storage tanks. Hard Arizona water scales these systems quickly, so we offer descaling and flushing on a schedule. We also install mixing valves to deliver safe temperatures at handwashing sinks.

    Drain Jetting and Sewer Camera Work in Tucson

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper and debris that build up faster than in homes. We use high-pressure jetters to scour main lines and camera equipment to locate breaks, roots and bellies. Video records help you plan repairs and budget for capital work. Preventive jetting reduces emergency backups.
