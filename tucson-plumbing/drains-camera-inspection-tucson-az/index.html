    Detailed Drains Camera Inspection Services in Tucson, AZ

    Main Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Tucson

    A main sewer camera inspection follows the line from your cleanout to the city tap or septic tank. We look for root intrusion, cracked joints, offset pipe, bellies and failing connections. The camera's sonde transmits a signal so we can mark the exact spot on your lawn or driveway. You receive a recording and notes that any contractor can use.

    Pre-Purchase Sewer Scope in Tucson, AZ

    Standard home inspections usually do not include the sewer line, yet sewer repairs can cost thousands. A pre-purchase sewer scope shows the condition of the lateral before you close. Older homes with clay or Orangeburg pipe benefit most. Our findings can support repair requests or price negotiations with the seller.

    Kitchen and Branch Drain Camera Inspection in Tucson

    Smaller cameras fit through kitchen, bathroom and laundry lines inside the house. They help explain recurring clogs and slow drains caused by grease buildup, scale or a broken fitting under the slab. We can see where branch lines tie into the main and whether they slope correctly. This is especially useful before a kitchen or bathroom remodel.

    Camera Inspection for Slab Leaks and Under-Slab Drains in Tucson, AZ

    Most Arizona homes are built on slab foundations, so drains run under concrete where they cannot be seen. Cracked or separated under-slab drains can leak into the soil and contribute to foundation movement. A camera shows the location of the break and lets us plan the smallest possible access cut or a reroute. This avoids tearing up more flooring than necessary.

    Post-Repair and Post-Jetting Verification in Tucson

    After a root cutting, hydro jetting or pipe repair, a follow-up camera pass confirms the work was done right. You see that the roots are gone, the grease is cleared or the new section is properly aligned. This step gives peace of mind and documents the condition for future reference. It is also helpful when a warranty or landlord agreement requires proof.

    Camera Inspections for Insurance and HOA Claims in Tucson, AZ

    When a sewer backup or drain leak causes damage, insurers often want proof of the cause. Our recorded video and written findings document roots, breaks or blockages clearly. HOAs and property managers also use our reports to decide who is responsible for a shared line. We provide files in a format that is easy to share.

    Commercial Drain Camera Inspection in Tucson

    Restaurants, apartment buildings and offices rely on drain systems that see heavy daily use. We inspect larger lines, grease waste lines and shared building drains. Scheduled inspections help catch buildup and damage before they cause a shutdown. Work can be scheduled outside business hours.

    Camera Inspection Cost vs. Exploratory Digging in Tucson, AZ

    A camera inspection usually costs a few hundred dollars, while exploratory digging can run much higher and still miss the problem. With a camera and locator, we know where to dig and how deep before a shovel touches the ground. That keeps excavation small and restoration costs low. Many homeowners find the inspection pays for itself.
