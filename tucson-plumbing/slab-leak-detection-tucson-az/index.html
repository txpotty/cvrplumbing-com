    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Tucson

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs constantly and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before marking the spot.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Tucson, AZ

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are harder to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards. We isolate the cold side and use acoustic equipment to listen for the leak through the slab. Line tracers map the pipe route so we know where to listen.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Tucson

    Many newer Arizona homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak safely or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.

    Slab Leak Repair Through Direct Access in Tucson, AZ

    When a single leak is found on otherwise healthy pipe, direct access can be the most economical repair. We cut a small opening in the floor at the marked spot, repair the pipe and pressure test it. Then we patch the concrete and leave the area ready for flooring. Accurate detection keeps that opening as small as possible.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Tucson

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in Tucson, AZ

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Tucson

    A slab leak that is flooding floors or causing water to bubble up through tile needs fast attention. We respond quickly, help you shut off the water and isolate the leaking side if possible. That often lets you keep partial water service until the repair. Then we locate and fix the leak.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair Cost Comparison in Tucson, AZ

    Slab leak detection typically costs $150 to $450. Direct access repairs usually run $1,000 to $3,000, including concrete patching but not flooring replacement. Reroutes generally cost $800 to $3,500 depending on distance and access. Full repipes cost more, often $4,000 to $15,000, but end the problem for good.
