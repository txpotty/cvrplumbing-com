    Comprehensive Plumbing Maintenance Services in Phoenix, AZ

    Water Heater Maintenance in Phoenix

    Hard water leaves sediment in the bottom of tank water heaters, which lowers efficiency and can cause popping or rumbling sounds. We flush the tank, check the anode rod, test the T&P valve and inspect the venting and gas or electric connections. Tankless units get a descaling flush with a pump and vinegar or descaler solution through the service valves. Regular service can help your heater reach or exceed its expected lifespan. It also helps keep the manufacturer warranty valid.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Phoenix, AZ

    A water softener protects every pipe and appliance from hard water scale, but it needs care to keep working. We check salt levels, clean the brine tank, inspect the resin bed and test the regeneration cycle. We also verify the bypass valve and drain line are working properly. A softener that is not regenerating correctly lets hard water through without you noticing. Routine service keeps your water soft and your equipment protected.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Phoenix

    Many homes here are built on concrete slabs with water lines running underneath. Soil movement and hard water can wear these lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents slab damage and mold.

    Sewer Line and Drain Camera Inspection in Phoenix, AZ

    A camera inspection shows the condition of your sewer line and main drains. We look for roots, bellies, cracks and buildup before they cause a backup. Older homes with clay or cast iron lines benefit most from regular checks. We record the inspection and give you a copy. If cleaning or repair is needed, we explain your options.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Phoenix

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many Arizona water providers require annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Phoenix, AZ

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a written estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and peace of mind.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Phoenix

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get replaced. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility first.
