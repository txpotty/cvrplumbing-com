    Leaking Disposal Replacement in Chandler

    When a disposal leaks from the body, the internal seal has usually failed. This cannot be repaired. We remove the leaking unit, inspect the cabinet for water damage, and install a new disposal. Then we test it with water and check for leaks.

    InSinkErator Replacement in Chandler, AZ

    If you already have an InSinkErator, replacement is usually quick because the mounting system often matches. We can upgrade from a Badger to an Evolution model for quieter operation and better grinding. We follow the brand's specs for wiring and drains. We also register the warranty for you.

    Upgrading to a Quieter Disposal in Chandler

    Older disposals can be loud and vibrate the sink. Newer models include sound insulation and anti-vibration mounts. We help you choose a quieter unit and install it carefully. Many customers are surprised at how much quieter their kitchen becomes.

    Higher Horsepower Disposal Upgrades in Chandler, AZ

    If your old disposal struggles with food waste, upgrading to a 3/4 or 1 HP unit can help. More power means fewer jams and better grinding. We confirm your electrical supply can handle the new unit. Then we install and test it.

    Mount and Flange Replacement in Chandler

    During replacement, we inspect the sink flange and mount. If they are corroded or leaking, we replace them with new parts. This prevents leaks at the top of the disposal. It also helps the new unit seal tightly.

    Disposal Replacement with Dishwasher Connection in Chandler, AZ

    If your dishwasher drains through the disposal, we reconnect the drain hose and remove the knockout plug on the new unit. We also check for an air gap or high loop. This prevents backflow into the dishwasher. We test both appliances after the install.

    Emergency Disposal Replacement in Chandler

    If your disposal is leaking onto the floor or backing up the sink, turn off the power and call us. We can often replace it the same day. Quick replacement prevents damage to cabinets and floors. We carry common models on our trucks.

    Disposal Replacement Cost and Value in Chandler, AZ

    Replacement costs depend on the model and any needed drain or electrical changes. A basic unit swap is the least expensive. Upgrades to quieter, stronger models cost more but last longer and work better. We provide clear pricing so you can choose.
