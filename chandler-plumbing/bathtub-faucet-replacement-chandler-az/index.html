    Tub Spout Replacement in Chandler

    Tub spouts crack, corrode and lose their diverter function over time. We match slip-on or threaded spouts to your pipe and replace them with a correct-length nipple when needed. A new spout stops water from shooting out while the shower is on. It is a quick fix with a big improvement in function.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Chandler, AZ

    Many dripping tub faucets just need a new cartridge or stem and seat. We replace Moen 1222 and 1225 cartridges, Delta single-handle cartridges, and two-handle stems for older brands. We also clean scale from the valve body so the new part seals. This often costs far less than a full valve swap.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Valve Conversion in Chandler

    Many older homes still have three-handle tub and shower faucets. We remove the old valve, install a modern single-handle pressure-balance valve, and cover the old holes with a large remodel plate. This upgrade adds scald protection and simplifies cleaning. Tile damage is usually limited to what the trim plate covers.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Chandler, AZ

    Thermostatic valves hold water temperature steady even when someone runs a sink or flushes a toilet. They are ideal for families and primary baths with large soaking tubs. We install models from Moen, Delta, Grohe and Hansgrohe and set the temperature limit. Precise control makes every bath more comfortable.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Chandler

    Freestanding tubs need floor-mount fillers with supply lines run through the slab or floor. Deck-mount fillers sit on the tub rim or surround. We plan the rough-in, set the valve and secure the filler so it does not wobble. These installs often happen during larger bathroom remodels.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Chandler, AZ

    When water keeps running from the spout while the shower is on, the diverter is likely failing. Some diverters are on the spout and some are built into the valve. We find which type you have and replace it. A working diverter brings back strong shower pressure.

    Emergency Tub Faucet Leak Service in Chandler

    A broken stem or handle can leave water running nonstop into the tub. If you cannot shut it off at the valve stops, turn off the main and call us. We prioritize runaway faucets and carry common stems and cartridges. Quick action prevents wasted water and high bills.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Chandler, AZ

    A cartridge replacement typically costs much less than replacing the valve in the wall. Valve replacement makes sense when parts are discontinued, the body is corroded, or you want scald protection. We explain what each option costs and how long each will last. You choose the approach that fits your budget and your bathroom plans.
