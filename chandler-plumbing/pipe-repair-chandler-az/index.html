    Professional Pipe Repair Services in Chandler, AZ

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Chandler

    Pinhole leaks form when copper pipe wall thins from the inside, often due to water chemistry, high velocity or poor grounding. They start as tiny drips that stain drywall or ceilings. We cut out the affected section and replace it with new pipe or a PEX transition. If we find several pinholes in the same area, we will talk with you about repiping that zone.

    Burst Pipe Repair in Chandler, AZ

    Pipes burst from overpressure, physical damage or the occasional hard freeze in higher-elevation parts of the state. A burst line can release gallons per minute, so shutting off the main fast is critical. We repair the damaged section, check nearby pipe for weakness and verify system pressure. We also help you document the damage for insurance.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Chandler

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in older homes and rusts from the inside until flow drops and leaks start at threaded joints. Repairs are possible, but every disturbed fitting can start a new leak. We replace failing sections with PEX or copper using dielectric unions to stop galvanic corrosion. For homes with widespread galvanized piping, we will quote a full repipe so you can compare.

    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Chandler, AZ

    PEX and CPVC are common in Arizona homes built over the last few decades. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic. CPVC becomes brittle with age and heat and can crack when bumped. We replace failed fittings and brittle sections with modern components rated for your system.

    Drain and Waste Pipe Repair in Chandler

    Drain pipes made of ABS, PVC or cast iron can crack, separate or leak at joints. A leaking drain often shows up as a stain below a bathroom or a musty smell in a cabinet. We locate the leak, replace the damaged section and test the drain under full flow. We also check for proper slope and venting while the line is open.

    Emergency Pipe Repair in Chandler, AZ

    When water is pouring through a ceiling or spraying under a sink, you need help right away. We walk you through shutting off the water over the phone and dispatch a plumber as quickly as possible. On arrival we stop the leak, make the repair and restore water. If a permanent repair needs more time, we install a safe temporary fix so you have water in the meantime.

    Commercial Pipe Repair in Chandler

    Offices, restaurants, retail spaces and apartment buildings depend on working plumbing to stay open. We repair supply and drain piping in commercial buildings, including larger diameter copper and cast iron lines. We schedule around business hours when needed and isolate only the affected area. Property managers get clear documentation of each repair.

    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Chandler, AZ

    A single pipe repair is much less expensive than a whole-house repipe and makes sense when the rest of the system is in good shape. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can quickly exceed repiping. We look at the age and condition of your plumbing and give you honest numbers for both paths. That way you can choose based on facts rather than pressure.
