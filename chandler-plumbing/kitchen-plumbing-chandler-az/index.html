    Full-Service Kitchen Plumbing Solutions in Chandler, AZ

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Chandler

    We install undermount, farmhouse, drop-in and workstation kitchen sinks with new strainers, traps and supply lines. Each sink is supported, sealed and connected to a properly vented drain. We can also add a disposal, filtered water faucet or dishwasher connection during the same visit. Our work coordinates with countertop installers for a smooth remodel.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Chandler, AZ

    Jammed, humming or leaking disposals are some of the most common kitchen calls we get. We free jams, reset motors, replace leaking seals and check the drain connection. If the unit is beyond repair, we will tell you and quote a replacement. Many repairs are completed in less than an hour.

    Garbage Disposal Installation and Replacement in Chandler

    We install new disposals and replace worn ones with models from InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen. We match horsepower to your household, from 1/3 HP to 1 HP units for heavy use. Each install includes a new mount, discharge tube and dishwasher knockout when needed. We test the unit and check for leaks before leaving.

    InSinkErator Installation in Chandler, AZ

    InSinkErator is one of the most popular disposal brands, and we install the full line from Badger to Evolution series. We use the brand's quick-lock mounting and follow its specs for electrical and drain connections. Upgrading to a quieter Evolution model is a common request. We also register the warranty for you.

    Grease Trap Installation in Chandler

    Restaurants and food service businesses need properly sized grease traps or interceptors to keep grease out of the sewer. We install under-sink grease traps and larger in-ground interceptors to match your flow rate and local requirements. We coordinate with your contractor and city inspector. A correct install prevents backups and fines.

    Grease Trap Cleaning and Repair in Chandler, AZ

    Grease traps need regular cleaning to work correctly. We clean small under-sink traps, inspect baffles and gaskets, and repair cracked lids or inlet tees. For larger interceptors, we coordinate pumping and inspect the structure. Keeping a maintenance log helps with health and pretreatment inspections.

    Dishwasher and Ice Maker Hookups in Chandler

    New dishwashers need a hot water line, a drain to the disposal or tailpiece and an air gap or high loop. Refrigerator ice makers need a dedicated cold line with a shutoff. We install both with braided or copper lines and test for leaks. This prevents the slow drips that ruin cabinet bottoms and floors.

    Kitchen Drain Clog Clearing in Chandler, AZ

    Kitchen clogs usually come from grease, starches and coffee grounds building up in the trap and branch line. We clear them with drain machines sized for kitchen lines and can camera-inspect stubborn clogs. Chemical drain cleaners often make grease clogs worse, so we avoid them. We also share tips to keep your kitchen drain clear.
