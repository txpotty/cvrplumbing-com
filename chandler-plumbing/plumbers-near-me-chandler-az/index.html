    Leak Detection and Pipe Repair in Chandler

    Hidden leaks waste water and can damage drywall, cabinets and flooring. We use acoustic listening devices, thermal cameras and pressure tests to find leaks without unnecessary demolition. Repairs range from replacing a single copper fitting to rerouting a section of PEX. We also check for pinhole leaks, which are common in older copper lines exposed to aggressive water.

    Drain Cleaning and Sewer Service in Chandler, AZ

    Clogged sinks, tubs and toilets are among the most common calls we take. We clear drains with cables and jetters sized to the pipe, then test for full flow. For repeat or whole-house backups, we inspect the main sewer line with a camera. We explain what caused the clog and how to prevent it.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Chandler

    Hard water causes sediment to build up quickly in Arizona tank water heaters, shortening their life. We flush tanks, replace anode rods, thermocouples and elements and install new tank or tankless units. Our plumbers install brands such as Rheem, Bradford White, A. O. Smith, Navien and Rinnai. Every installation includes proper venting, expansion tank and code-required strapping.

    Toilet, Faucet and Fixture Repair in Chandler, AZ

    Running toilets, dripping faucets and leaky shower valves waste water and drive up your bill. We replace flappers, fill valves, cartridges and wax rings, or install new fixtures when repairs no longer make sense. Mineral buildup from hard water often wears out cartridges faster here, so we carry common replacements. We can also install water-efficient fixtures that meet current standards.

    Water Softener and Filtration Service in Chandler

    Arizona's hard water leaves scale on fixtures, inside water heaters and in pipes. We install and service water softeners, reverse osmosis drinking water systems and whole-house filters. Proper installation includes a bypass valve, drain connection and correct salt settings. Treated water helps your plumbing and appliances last longer.
