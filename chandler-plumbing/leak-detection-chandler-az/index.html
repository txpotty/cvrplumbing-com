    Precise Leak Detection Services in Chandler, AZ

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leak Detection in Chandler

    Leaks inside walls and ceilings often show up as stains, bubbling paint, soft drywall or a musty smell. We use thermal cameras to spot temperature differences caused by water, then confirm with moisture meters. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line. Pinpointing the source lets us cut a small, clean access opening.

    Slab Leak Detection in Chandler, AZ

    Most Arizona homes sit on concrete slabs with water lines running underneath. Signs of a slab leak include warm floor spots, cracked tile, a running water meter and the sound of water with everything off. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests, then trace and listen to the line through the slab. Once located, we discuss spot repair, reroute or repipe options.

    Main Water Line Leak Detection in Chandler

    The service line between your meter and house can leak underground for months, especially in older galvanized or copper lines. Wet spots in the yard, unusually green grass or a sinking area near the front of the house are clues. We use line tracers to map the pipe and ground microphones to hear the leak. That keeps excavation to a small, targeted hole.

    Irrigation and Yard Leak Detection in Chandler, AZ

    Drip and sprinkler systems are a common source of hidden water loss in Arizona landscaping. A cracked lateral or a stuck valve can waste thousands of gallons before anyone notices. We test the irrigation system separately from the house and trace leaks along buried lines. Many water providers recommend checking irrigation first when bills spike in summer.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Chandler

    Arizona heat evaporates a lot of pool water, so it can be hard to tell normal loss from a leak. A bucket test compares evaporation to actual water loss. We then pressure test plumbing lines and use dye testing around lights, skimmers and fittings. Finding the leak protects the pool shell and the soil around it.

    Fixture and Appliance Leak Detection in Chandler, AZ

    Running toilets, worn faucet cartridges and leaking water heater connections waste more water than most people realize. A silent toilet leak can lose hundreds of gallons a day. We check flappers, fill valves, supply lines, softeners and water heater fittings. These repairs are usually quick and inexpensive.

    Commercial Leak Detection in Chandler

    Office buildings, retail centers and multifamily properties have long, complex supply systems where leaks can hide for a long time. We isolate zones, test each section and trace leaks with the same tools we use for homes. Property managers receive a written report for each unit or area tested. Early detection reduces tenant disruption and insurance claims.

    Leak Detection Cost Comparison in Chandler, AZ

    Professional leak detection typically costs $150 to $450 for a standard home, depending on the system size and how many areas need testing. Pool leak detection usually runs $250 to $500. Repair costs vary widely, from $150 for a fixture fix to several thousand dollars for a slab reroute. Accurate detection keeps the repair cost as low as possible.
