    Bathroom Faucet Installation in Chandler, AZ

    Touchless Faucet Installation in Chandler

    Touchless faucets from Moen, Delta and Kohler use sensors, solenoids and either batteries or a plug-in power supply. We mount the control box under the sink where it stays dry and accessible. We test sensor range and flow so the faucet responds reliably. Hard water can affect solenoids over time, so we add an inline filter when recommended.

    Wall-Mount Faucet Installation in Chandler, AZ

    Wall-mount faucets need a rough-in valve inside the wall, so they are best installed during a remodel. We set the valve at the right height and depth for your sink and finished wall surface. Once tile is complete, we install the trim and spout. This style frees counter space and gives a clean look.

    Pot Filler Installation in Chandler

    A pot filler near the range lets you fill large pots without carrying them across the kitchen. We run a new cold water line to the wall behind the cooktop and install a shutoff valve. The faucet is mounted securely and tested for leaks. It is a popular upgrade in kitchen remodels.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Installation in Chandler

    Labor for a standard kitchen or bathroom faucet installation usually costs $150 to $350. Touchless faucets, wall-mount faucets and pot fillers range from $300 to $800 or more depending on the work involved. We give you a flat price up front.

    Moen, Delta and Kohler are reliable choices with strong warranties and easy-to-find parts. Grohe and Brizo offer high-end options. We can suggest models based on your style and budget.

    Replacing a faucet does not typically require a permit. Adding new supply lines, like for a pot filler during a remodel, may be part of a larger permitted project.

    Touchless faucets are convenient and more hygienic in busy kitchens. They cost more and need batteries or power, but many homeowners find them worth the upgrade.

    Faucets carry manufacturer warranties, often lifetime for many brands. We guarantee our installation workmanship and will return if a leak is caused by our work.

    Most faucet installations take 45 minutes to 1.5 hours. Wall-mount faucets and pot fillers take longer because they require new rough-in plumbing.

    We strongly recommend it. Old supply lines can be brittle or corroded, and new braided stainless lines are inexpensive insurance against leaks.
