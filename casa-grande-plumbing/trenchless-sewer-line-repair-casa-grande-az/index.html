    Long-Lasting Trenchless Sewer Line Repair Services in Casa Grande, AZ

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Casa Grande

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their shape.

    Pipe Bursting in Casa Grande, AZ

    Pipe bursting replaces a badly damaged line by pulling a bursting head through the old pipe. The head breaks the old pipe outward while new HDPE pipe follows behind it. Bursting is ideal for collapsed or severely offset lines that cannot be lined. It usually requires an entry pit and an exit pit.

    Sectional Point Repair in Casa Grande

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Casa Grande, AZ

    Mesquite, palo verde, ficus and eucalyptus roots seek out moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause recurring backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every few months.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Casa Grande

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Casa Grande, AZ

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Repair in Casa Grande

    Restaurants, offices and multifamily buildings cannot afford long shutdowns or torn-up parking lots. Trenchless methods let us repair commercial sewer lines with minimal downtime and disruption. We schedule work around business hours when possible. Larger diameter lining and bursting are available for bigger lines.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Cost in Casa Grande, AZ

    Point repairs often cost $1,500 to $3,500, while full lining of a residential lateral commonly runs $4,000 to $12,000 depending on length and diameter. Pipe bursting typically ranges from $5,000 to $15,000. Traditional excavation can be cheaper upfront on short, shallow runs, but restoring concrete and landscaping can quickly close the gap. We price both options so you can compare.
