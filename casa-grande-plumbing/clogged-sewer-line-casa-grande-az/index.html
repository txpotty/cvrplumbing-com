    Proven Clogged Sewer Line Services in Casa Grande, AZ

    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Casa Grande

    Roots from mesquite, palo verde, ficus and eucalyptus trees seek out moisture and push into sewer pipe joints. Clay and older cast iron pipe are especially prone to root intrusion because of their many joints. We cut roots with a sewer machine fitted with root saws and then camera the line to see how much damage remains. For ongoing root problems, we can recommend a root treatment schedule or lining.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Casa Grande, AZ

    Cooking grease cools and hardens in the main line, and so-called flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. Together they form dense plugs that a cable may only punch a hole through. Hydro jetting at high pressure scours the pipe walls clean and flushes debris downstream. We also share simple habits that help prevent another grease or wipe clog.

    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Casa Grande

    Hydro jetting uses water at thousands of psi through a specialized nozzle to cut roots, grease and scale. It cleans the full diameter of the pipe rather than just boring a hole through the clog. We check the pipe condition with a camera first so jetting is only used on lines that can handle it. Many homeowners find that jetting buys them years before another clog.

    Sewer Belly and Sagging Pipe Clogs in Casa Grande, AZ

    Expansive clay soils in parts of Arizona swell and shrink, which can cause sections of sewer pipe to sink and form a belly. Waste and water collect in the low spot and lead to repeat clogs. Our camera and locator show where the belly is and how deep it sits. We can then quote a spot repair to restore proper slope.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Casa Grande

    Some older homes have no accessible cleanout, which forces plumbers to pull a toilet or go through a roof vent to clear the main line. We install a two-way cleanout near the foundation so future clearing is faster and cheaper. The cleanout also gives the city or a future plumber easy access for inspections. This is a common upgrade after a major sewer clog.

    Emergency Sewer Backup Service in Casa Grande, AZ

    Sewage coming up through a shower or floor drain needs immediate attention. Our emergency crews stop the backup, clear the line and help contain the mess. We can refer you to a cleanup and restoration crew if sewage reached carpet or drywall. We also provide photos and notes for insurance claims.

    Commercial Sewer Line Clearing in Casa Grande

    Restaurants, apartment complexes and retail centers move large volumes of waste through their sewer lines. We clear larger diameter lines, grease-heavy kitchen mains and shared building drains. Scheduled jetting and camera maintenance help prevent costly shutdowns. We can work overnight to keep your doors open.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Casa Grande, AZ

    If the camera shows a sound pipe with a grease or root clog, cleaning is usually all you need. Cracked, offset or collapsed pipe will keep clogging no matter how often it is cleared. Cleaning typically costs a few hundred dollars, while repairs range from a spot fix to trenchless lining or full replacement. We walk you through the footage and costs so you can choose.
