    Lasting Sewer Repair Services in Casa Grande, AZ

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Casa Grande

    Every sewer repair starts with a look inside the pipe. We run a self-leveling camera from your cleanout or a pulled toilet and record the full length of the line. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact location and depth of every problem on the surface. You get a copy of the video and a plain-English summary of what we found. This step alone often saves homeowners from paying for repairs they do not need.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Casa Grande, AZ

    Mesquite, palo verde, eucalyptus and ficus roots go straight for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than repeated cleaning.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Casa Grande

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Casa Grande, AZ

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. In Arizona, bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where expansive clay soil settled after heavy monsoon rain. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is usually a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Casa Grande

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can often use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Casa Grande, AZ

    A collapsed sewer line means every drain in the house can back up at once. When that happens, we respond fast, clear a temporary path for flow where possible and help you avoid using fixtures until the repair is done. We stage the excavation, make the repair and restore service as quickly as safe work allows. If sewage has entered living space, we will tell you honestly when you also need a cleanup company.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in Casa Grande

    Restaurants, retail centers, offices and multifamily buildings put far more load on a sewer lateral than a single-family home. Grease, wipes and high flow can crush or clog a weak line quickly. We repair larger diameter commercial laterals, install two-way cleanouts and coordinate after-hours work to limit downtime. We also provide inspection video for property managers and insurance claims.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Casa Grande, AZ

    A single spot repair is the most affordable fix when the rest of the line is sound. Pipe lining costs more per foot but avoids excavation and works well for long runs under driveways or landscaping. Full replacement is the right call when the pipe is Orangeburg, badly bellied or broken in several places. We lay out all three options with real numbers so you can weigh cost against how long the repair will last.
