    Modern Shower Replacement Services in Casa Grande, AZ

    Shower Valve Replacement in Casa Grande

    Old two- and three-handle shower valves often leak and lack anti-scald protection. We open the wall from the back or front, remove the old valve and install a modern pressure-balance or thermostatic valve. Where possible, we use a larger trim plate to cover the old opening. This upgrade improves safety and comfort.

    Shower Pan Replacement in Casa Grande, AZ

    A cracked or leaking shower pan can damage subfloors, walls and adjacent rooms. On slab homes, water can travel under flooring and cause staining and odors. We remove the old pan, repair the drain and install a new pan with proper slope and waterproofing. A flood test confirms the new pan is watertight.

    Fiberglass Shower Unit Replacement in Casa Grande

    Older fiberglass and acrylic units can crack, fade and leak at the base. We remove the old unit, check the framing and drain and install a new one-piece or multi-piece unit. Many homes have narrow doorways, so we often use multi-piece units that fit through. The result is a clean, durable shower.

    Tile Shower Replacement in Casa Grande, AZ

    Tile showers can fail when grout cracks or waterproofing breaks down. We handle the plumbing side of tile shower replacement, including valve, drain and pan. We coordinate with your tile installer so waterproofing membranes are installed correctly. Modern systems like Schluter Kerdi are popular choices.

    Tub-to-Shower Replacement in Casa Grande

    Replacing an old tub with a walk-in shower improves safety and accessibility. We remove the tub, adjust the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. We can add a handheld shower and grab bar blocking. This upgrade is popular for aging in place.

    Showerhead and Trim Upgrade in Casa Grande, AZ

    If the valve is sound, replacing the showerhead and trim can refresh the look without opening the wall. We match trim kits to your existing valve brand and install new heads, handhelds and slide bars. Hard water clogs older heads quickly, so we recommend models with easy-clean nozzles. This is the most affordable upgrade.

    Commercial and Rental Shower Replacement in Casa Grande

    Rentals, hotels and gyms need durable showers that are easy to maintain. We replace valves, heads and units with commercial-grade components. Work is scheduled to minimize downtime for tenants or guests. We also standardize parts to simplify future repairs.

    Shower Replacement Cost Comparison in Casa Grande, AZ

    A valve-only replacement usually costs far less than a full rebuild, but it only makes sense if the pan and walls are sound. Shower pan replacement often requires removing tile, which raises the cost. We compare the price of each option against the condition of your shower. That helps you invest in the fix that lasts.
