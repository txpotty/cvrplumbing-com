    InSinkErator Disposal Installation in Casa Grande, AZ

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Casa Grande

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. They do not need a wall switch. We install them with proper mounting and drain connections. They are a good choice for kitchens without a convenient switch location.

    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Casa Grande, AZ

    Air switches let you start the disposal with a button on the sink or countertop. This avoids drilling for a new wall switch. We install the button and control box under the sink. Air switches are popular in kitchen remodels and islands.

    Dishwasher Connection with Disposal Installation in Casa Grande

    When a dishwasher drains through the disposal, the knockout plug must be removed and the drain hose connected. We install an air gap or high loop to prevent backflow. This keeps dirty water from flowing back into the dishwasher. We test both appliances together.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Casa Grande, AZ

    Double bowl sinks need a disposal on one side and a continuous waste connection on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain well and the disposal fits under the sink. Proper slope prevents standing water. We use heavy-wall fittings for long life.

    Disposal Installation in Rentals and Investment Properties in Casa Grande

    Landlords often want reliable, affordable disposals that hold up to tenant use. We install durable models with strong warranties and simple maintenance needs. Our installs are quick to minimize turnover time. We can handle multiple units on one visit.
