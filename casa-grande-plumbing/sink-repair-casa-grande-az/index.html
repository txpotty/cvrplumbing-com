    Dependable Sink Repair Services in Casa Grande, AZ

    Leaking P-Trap Repair in Casa Grande

    Most puddles under a bathroom sink start at the P-trap. Slip-joint washers flatten over time, plastic traps crack, and old chrome traps rust through from the inside. We replace the trap with a properly sized 1-1/4 inch assembly, align the tailpiece and wall arm, and run water to confirm a dry seal. This is one of the quickest and least expensive fixes we do.

    Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in Casa Grande, AZ

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is usually to blame. The pivot rod nut can leak, the clevis strap can slip, and hair wraps around the stopper body. We clean or replace the assembly and adjust it so the lift rod works smoothly. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.

    Slow Drain and Clog Clearing in Casa Grande

    Bathroom sinks clog with hair, soap scum and toothpaste mixed with Arizona hard-water scale. We pull the stopper, clean the overflow channel, and clear the trap and branch line with a hand auger instead of harsh chemicals. If the clog sits past the wall, we check whether a shared line with the tub or shower is the real problem. You end up with a sink that drains at full speed.

    Supply Line and Shutoff Valve Repair in Casa Grande, AZ

    Braided supply lines and angle stops are rated for years, not decades, and the rubber inside breaks down in warm cabinets. A failing line can burst without warning and flood a bathroom. We replace worn lines with stainless braided connectors and swap stuck multi-turn valves for quarter-turn stops. It is a small job that prevents a very expensive one.

    Loose or Cracked Basin Repair in Casa Grande

    Undermount sinks are held by clips and epoxy that can let go, leaving a gap and a leak along the counter. Vessel sinks can rock on a worn mounting ring, and older porcelain basins can crack around the drain. We reattach, reseal or replace the basin as needed and recaulk with a mildew-resistant silicone. We will tell you honestly when a crack means replacement is the better value.

    Overflow and Drain Body Leak Repair in Casa Grande, AZ

    Water that appears only when the sink is full often comes from the overflow gasket or the drain flange putty. We pull the drain, clean the old putty or sealant, and reset the flange with fresh plumber's putty or silicone based on the basin material. Then we fill the bowl past the overflow and check for drips. This kind of leak is easy to miss and easy to fix once found.

    Emergency Sink Leak Repair in Casa Grande

    A burst supply line or a broken trap can put gallons on the floor in minutes. If you cannot stop the water at the angle stops, shut off the main and call us. We prioritize active leaks and carry the parts to stop them on the first visit. We also check the vanity floor and wall for moisture so damage does not spread.

    Sink Repair vs Replacement Costs in Casa Grande, AZ

    A trap, stopper or supply line repair typically runs a fraction of the cost of a new sink. Replacement starts to make sense when the basin is cracked, the finish is badly etched, or the drain body is corroded into the bowl. We give you both prices when it is close so you can decide. Many customers save money by repairing now and planning an upgrade later.
