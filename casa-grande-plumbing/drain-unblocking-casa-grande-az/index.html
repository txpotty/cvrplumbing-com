    Blocked Toilet Clearing in Casa Grande

    Toilets block from too much paper, flushable wipes, feminine products or solid objects. A closet auger with a protective sleeve clears most blockages without scratching the bowl. If an object is lodged in the trapway, we pull the toilet to retrieve it. We reset it with a new wax ring and test several flushes. Low-flow toilets with narrow trapways can block more easily, and we can advise on upgrades.

    Blocked Kitchen Sink Unblocking in Casa Grande, AZ

    A kitchen sink full of standing water usually means grease and food have sealed the line. If a disposal is involved, we check that it is not jammed and that the outlet is clear. We remove the trap and run a drum machine into the wall line. Once the blockage is out, we flush with hot water. We also share simple tips to keep grease out of the line.

    Blocked Shower and Tub Drains in Casa Grande

    Hair and soap scum are the main causes of blocked shower and tub drains. We remove the drain cover or stopper and pull the clog out by hand or with a small auger. For deeper blocks, we run a cable through the overflow opening. We check the drain assembly for loose parts that trap hair. Your shower drains properly before we leave.

    Blocked Bathroom Sink Unblocking in Casa Grande, AZ

    Bathroom sinks block with hair, toothpaste and soap mixed with hard water minerals. We clean the pop-up stopper and P-trap first, which often solves the problem. If the blockage is in the wall, we run a small cable into the branch. Old or corroded traps can be replaced while we are there. We finish by testing the sink and checking for leaks.

    Main Line Blockage Unblocking in Casa Grande

    If multiple drains are blocked or water comes up in the tub when you flush, the blockage is likely in the main line. We find the cleanout and run a heavy cable with a cutting head toward the street. Roots, wipes and grease are the usual causes. Once flow is restored, a camera inspection can show what is going on in the pipe. We recommend next steps based on what we see.
