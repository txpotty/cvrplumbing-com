    Reliable Water Heater Replacement Services in Tempe, AZ

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Tempe

    Gas water heaters recover hot water faster than electric models and are common in Arizona homes with natural gas service. We replace the old tank, install a new flexible gas connector and sediment trap, and confirm the vent is properly sized and pitched. Atmospheric and power-vent models are available depending on your venting options. We light the burner and check combustion and draft before leaving.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Tempe, AZ

    Electric water heaters are common in homes without gas service and in many condos and apartments. We replace the tank, connect wiring to the existing circuit and check that breaker and wire size match the new unit's elements. Hard water often causes element failure, so we recommend models with scale-resistant elements. Hybrid heat pump models are also available for higher efficiency.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Tempe

    Many Arizona homes, especially those built in certain eras, have water heaters in the attic. Replacing an attic unit requires careful lifting, a drain pan with a drain line and safe access for future service. We evaluate whether relocating the heater to the garage or an exterior closet makes sense to reduce water damage risk. Our crews protect your ceilings and floors during removal and installation.

    Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith in Tempe, AZ

    We install trusted brands including Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith. Bradford White is sold through professional plumbers and is known for durable construction. Rheem and A.O. Smith offer a wide range of capacities and efficiency options. We help you compare warranties, features and price to find the right fit.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Tempe

    If you are running out of hot water, your household may have outgrown your current tank. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can solve the problem. We look at bathroom count, fixture usage and household size to recommend the right capacity. Upsizing may require venting or gas line adjustments, which we include in your quote.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Tempe, AZ

    A leaking tank can flood a garage or closet quickly and damage drywall, cabinets and flooring. If your heater is leaking, shut off the cold water supply valve and turn off the gas or power. We respond quickly and can often replace the heater the same day. We also check surrounding areas for water damage.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Tempe

    Restaurants, salons, offices and multi-unit buildings rely on commercial water heaters with higher capacity and recovery rates. We replace commercial tanks and coordinate shutoffs to minimize downtime. Larger units may need upgraded venting, gas supply and expansion tanks. We provide documentation for inspections and property records.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in Tempe, AZ

    A standard tank replacement is usually the most affordable option, while tankless and hybrid heat pump models cost more upfront but can reduce energy use. Attic installs, relocations and code upgrades add to the price. We provide flat-rate quotes for each option so you can compare total cost, efficiency and warranty side by side. Many homeowners choose based on budget, space and how long they plan to stay in the home.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Tempe

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and common code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years in Arizona because of hard water and sediment buildup. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help extend that life.

    Manufacturer tank warranties typically range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    Most jurisdictions require a permit for water heater replacement. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection when required.
