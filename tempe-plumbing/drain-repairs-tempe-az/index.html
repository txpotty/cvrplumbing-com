    Dependable Drain Repair Services in Tempe, AZ

    Kitchen Sink Drain Repair in Tempe

    Kitchen drains handle grease, food scraps and garbage disposal discharge, which makes them the most clog-prone line in most homes. We replace corroded or cracked tailpieces, fix leaking slip nuts and correct traps installed too low or too high. If grease has built up deep in the branch line, we clean it and check the slope so it does not return. We also repair the drain connection to your dishwasher and disposal.

    Bathroom Drain Repair in Tempe, AZ

    Hair, soap scum and hard water minerals combine to narrow bathroom drains over time. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies. For tubs and showers with failed drain gaskets, we can often repair from above without opening the ceiling below. When access is needed, we make the smallest opening possible and keep it neat for patching.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Repair in Tempe

    Washing machines dump a large volume of water fast, and an undersized or poorly vented standpipe will overflow. We correct standpipe height, replace cracked laundry box drains and add proper venting. For garage and utility floor drains, we restore the trap seal so sewer odor does not escape. We also repair the trap primers that keep those seals filled.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Tempe, AZ

    Many older homes have cast iron drain stacks and branch lines that are now corroding from the inside. Rust scale catches debris and cracks let water seep into walls and floors. We cut out failing sections and replace them with ABS or PVC using shielded transition couplings. When the whole stack is failing, we quote a full replacement so you can plan the work.

    Drain Vent Repair in Tempe

    A drain that gurgles, drains slowly or lets sewer gas into the room may have a blocked or missing vent. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block vent pipes on the roof. We clear or repair the vent, and where a vent was never installed we can add an air admittance valve where local code permits it. Proper venting protects every trap seal in the house.

    Emergency Drain Repair in Tempe, AZ

    A drain line that bursts under a sink or inside a wall can soak cabinets and flooring quickly. When you call with an active leak, we prioritize the visit and help you stop the flow until we arrive. Once there, we isolate the damaged section and make a permanent repair whenever possible. If drywall or flooring is wet, we let you know so you can address it before mold sets in.

    Commercial Drain Repair in Tempe

    Restaurants, salons, offices and medical suites all need drains that keep working during business hours. We repair grease-laden kitchen lines, floor sinks, mop sink drains and restroom fixtures. We can schedule work early or after closing to keep your doors open. Our team also helps you set up a maintenance plan so problems are caught before they disrupt service.

    Drain Repair Cost Comparison in Tempe, AZ

    A trap or slip-joint repair is one of the least expensive plumbing jobs and is often done in under an hour. Replacing a section of branch drain in an open wall or crawlspace costs more but is still a modest repair. Opening finished walls or replacing a full stack is the most involved option. We explain each choice and its price so you can decide with confidence.
