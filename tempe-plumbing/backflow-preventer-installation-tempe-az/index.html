    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Tempe

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. Fertilizer, herbicides and stagnant water in sprinkler and drip lines can be siphoned into your drinking water if pressure drops. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the layout of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Tempe, AZ

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry trusted brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Tempe

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Tempe, AZ

    Pools are common in Arizona, and an auto-fill line or hose connection creates a direct path for chlorinated water to reach the supply. We install the correct device on pool fill lines and check that hose bibbs have vacuum breakers. This simple step protects your household water and keeps you in line with local code. We can add the work when a pool is being built or remodeled.

    Commercial Backflow Preventer Installation in Tempe

    Restaurants, salons, dental offices and light industrial buildings often need multiple backflow devices on soda machines, boilers, dishwashers and process water. We survey the building, identify each cross-connection and install containment and isolation assemblies as required. Work is scheduled after hours or on weekends when needed to avoid shutting down your business. You receive documentation for each device for your records and your water provider.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Tempe, AZ

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights in higher elevation areas. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Tempe

    Most Arizona water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it on the spot with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.

    Backflow Preventer Installation Cost Comparison in Tempe, AZ

    A residential pressure vacuum breaker is the most affordable option, while larger RPZ assemblies for commercial buildings cost significantly more in parts and labor. Size, brand, enclosure needs and how much existing pipe must be modified all affect the price. Replacing a failed old device with a new one is often cheaper than repeated rebuilds. We give you an itemized quote that includes the initial certification test.
