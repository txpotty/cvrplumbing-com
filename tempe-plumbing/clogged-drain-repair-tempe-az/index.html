    Thorough Clogged Drain Repair Services in Tempe, AZ

    Kitchen Sink Drain Clogs in Tempe

    Kitchen drains clog from cooking grease, coffee grounds and starchy food that stick to the pipe walls and harden. Garbage disposals can push food further down the line where it collects in horizontal runs. We remove the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and branch line and clear the buildup back to the main stack. For heavy grease, we may follow with a small jetter to wash the walls clean.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Tempe, AZ

    Bathroom sinks clog from hair, toothpaste and soap that bind together on the pop-up stopper and in the P-trap. Hard water adds a mineral crust that makes the pipe narrower over time. We pull and clean the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable through the wall line. We can also replace worn pop-up assemblies and corroded tailpieces while we are there.

    Tub and Shower Drain Clogs in Tempe

    Hair and soap scum are the most common causes of slow tubs and showers. We remove the strainer or overflow plate and pull the clog out with a drum auger designed for small diameter drains. If the drain is still slow, we check the trap under the slab or in the access panel. We take care not to scratch acrylic, fiberglass or porcelain finishes.

    Laundry and Utility Drain Clogs in Tempe, AZ

    Washing machines discharge a lot of water quickly, and lint, fabric fibers and detergent residue build up in the standpipe and branch line. A clogged laundry drain often overflows at the standpipe mid-cycle. We clear the line with a cable and recommend a lint trap for the discharge hose. In some homes the laundry line ties into the kitchen line, so we check both.

    Floor Drain and Garage Drain Clearing in Tempe

    Floor drains in garages, utility rooms and commercial kitchens collect dirt, sand and debris, especially after monsoon storms track grit indoors. When their traps dry out in the Arizona heat, sewer gas can also escape. We clear the drain, flush the trap and can install trap primers or trap seals to keep odors out.

    Recurring Drain Clogs and Camera Diagnosis in Tempe, AZ

    If the same drain clogs every few weeks, the problem is often a sagging pipe, a broken fitting or heavy scale. We run a small inspection camera through the line to see the inside of the pipe. You see the footage on screen with us. That lets you decide whether a deeper cleaning, a spot repair or a section replacement makes the most sense.

    Emergency Clogged Drain Service in Tempe

    A backed up drain right before guests arrive or during a holiday meal is not something that can wait. Our emergency technicians respond quickly with full drain equipment. If several fixtures are backing up at once, we check the main line too, since that usually means the clog is further downstream.

    Commercial Drain Clog Clearing in Tempe, AZ

    Restaurants, salons and offices see heavy drain use and need fast, dependable service. We clear grease-heavy kitchen lines, hair-clogged salon sinks and mop sinks. Preventive jetting schedules are available to keep lines open between visits. We can work early or late to avoid interrupting your business.
