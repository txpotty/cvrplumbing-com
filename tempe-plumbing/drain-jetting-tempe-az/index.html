    Effective Drain Jetting Services in Tempe, AZ

    Kitchen Drain Jetting in Tempe

    Kitchen lines are where grease, food particles and dish soap collect and harden. Over time the pipe narrows until the sink drains slowly or backs up into the other basin. We access the line through a cleanout or the trap arm and feed a small jetting hose toward the main. Rear-facing jets scrub the walls while the stream flushes residue away. Your sink and dishwasher drain at full speed again.

    Laundry Drain Jetting in Tempe, AZ

    Washing machines discharge lint, fabric softener residue and fine sand, especially in homes near open desert land. These build into a gray sludge that slows the standpipe and overflows during the spin cycle. Jetting clears the standpipe and branch line completely. We also check the trap, vent and drain size to confirm they can handle the washer flow. A lint filter on the discharge hose helps keep the line clean.

    Bathroom Branch Line Jetting in Tempe

    Bathroom drains collect hair, soap scum and mineral scale from hard water. When several bathroom fixtures drain slowly together, the shared branch line may be coated. We jet the branch to remove buildup that a hand auger cannot reach. This helps restore drainage to sinks, tubs and showers on the same line. We test each fixture before leaving.

    Restaurant and Commercial Drain Jetting in Tempe, AZ

    Commercial kitchens send large amounts of grease into floor sinks, prep sinks and dish lines. Even with a grease interceptor, branch lines inside the building build up quickly. We offer scheduled jetting to keep these lines open and avoid shutdowns during service hours. Work can be done before opening or after closing. Service records are provided for your files.

    Drain Jetting for Hard Water Scale in Tempe

    Hard water leaves mineral deposits inside drain pipes, particularly in older galvanized and cast iron. That rough scale traps hair, grease and soap, leading to frequent clogs. A jetting nozzle designed for scale can break up and wash away much of this buildup. We use the camera to judge how much wall remains before and after. Clean pipe drains better and clogs less often.

    Floor Drain and Utility Sink Jetting in Tempe, AZ

    Garage floor drains, utility sinks and mop sinks often collect dirt, paint residue and debris. They are easy to forget until they back up. Jetting clears the line and flushes the trap. We also check the trap primer or seal to keep sewer gas out. This is a quick fix that prevents bigger problems.

    Drain Jetting Maintenance Plans in Tempe

    If you have a busy kitchen or a line that keeps clogging, scheduled jetting can keep it clear. Homes may need service once a year, while restaurants may need it every few months. Regular service prevents surprise backups and costly emergency calls. Maintenance plan customers get priority scheduling and set pricing. We keep records of every visit.

    Drain Jetting Cost Versus Snaking in Tempe, AZ

    Jetting a branch line costs more than a quick snaking visit. But if you are calling for the same clog several times a year, jetting usually saves money over time. It removes the cause instead of just opening a path. For a one-time hair clog, a cable may be all you need. We recommend the option that fits your situation and show you both prices.
