    Touchless Faucet Installation in Lake Havasu City

    Touchless faucets from Moen, Delta and Kohler use sensors, solenoids and either batteries or a plug-in power supply. We mount the control box under the sink where it stays dry and accessible.     Wall-Mount Faucet Installation in Lake Havasu City, AZ
