    Backflow Testing and Repair in Lake Havasu City

    Most Arizona water providers require annual testing of backflow prevention assemblies on commercial properties. We test reduced pressure zone, double check and pressure vacuum breaker assemblies with calibrated gauges. When a device fails, we rebuild it with manufacturer kits from Watts, Wilkins or Febco. We submit the results to your water provider so you stay in compliance.

    Grease Trap and Interceptor Service in Lake Havasu City, AZ

    Restaurants and food service sites must keep grease out of the public sewer. We inspect and service grease traps and interceptors, replace baffles and clear the lines leading to them. Hydro jetting removes hardened grease from kitchen drains that cables cannot clear. Regular service helps you pass health inspections and avoid sewer backups during dinner rush.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Lake Havasu City

    Commercial Water Heaters and Boilers in Lake Havasu City, AZ
