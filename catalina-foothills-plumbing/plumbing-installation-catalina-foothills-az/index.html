    Water Heater Installation in Catalina Foothills

    Hard water and high summer temperatures can shorten water heater life, so replacement is a common need. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon tank water heaters from trusted brands like Rheem, A.O. Smith and Bradford White. Each install includes new supply connectors, a code-compliant temperature and pressure relief line and proper venting. We also check whether your home needs an expansion tank. Most water heater installs are completed in one visit.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    Tankless water heaters from Navien, Rinnai and Rheem deliver endless hot water and save space in garages and closets. Gas units usually need a larger gas line and new venting, while electric units often need added amperage. We size the unit by flow rate and incoming water temperature. Because incoming water is often warm in summer, tankless units can perform well here. We also install isolation valves for future descaling.

    Water Softener Installation in Catalina Foothills

    Arizona water is very hard, which leaves scale on fixtures, appliances and water heaters. We install salt-based softeners, salt-free conditioners and combination filter systems. Many local homes have a softener loop in the garage, which simplifies installation. If your home lacks a loop, we can add one. We set the system to your water hardness and household size.

    Gas Line Installation in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    We install gas lines for water heaters, ranges, dryers, fireplaces, pool heaters and outdoor kitchens. Lines are sized based on BTU demand and pipe length so appliances get enough fuel. We use black iron or CSST depending on the application and code. Every new line is pressure tested before it is put into service. We also coordinate with the gas utility when meter changes are needed.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Catalina Foothills

    PEX is flexible, resistant to scale and a popular choice for repiping older homes with failing copper or galvanized pipe. We install PEX with manifold or home-run layouts for balanced pressure. The material handles hard water well and can reduce noise in the walls. We patch access openings neatly or coordinate with your drywall contractor. A PEX repipe can stop recurring pinhole leaks.

    Water Line Installation in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    We install new main water service lines from the meter to the house. Caliche and rocky soil can make trenching difficult, so we bring the right equipment for the job. We use approved materials like PEX or copper and install a new shutoff valve where needed. Proper depth and bedding protect the line from damage. We also test pressure and flow after installation.

    Backflow Preventer Installation in Catalina Foothills

    Backflow preventers keep contaminated water from flowing back into the public supply. They are commonly required for irrigation systems, fire lines and commercial buildings. We install reduced pressure, double check and pressure vacuum breaker assemblies as required. Each device is tested after installation by a certified tester. We can also help with annual testing requirements.

    Expansion Tank and Sewage Ejector Installs in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    Water heater expansion tanks absorb thermal expansion in closed systems, which protects pipes and relief valves. Homes with a pressure reducing valve often need one. We also install sewage ejector pumps for basements, guest suites and fixtures below the sewer line. These systems include a sealed basin, check valve and proper venting. Both installations protect your home from costly water damage.
