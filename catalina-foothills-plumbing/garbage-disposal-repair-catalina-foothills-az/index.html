    Jammed Disposal Repair in Catalina Foothills

    A humming disposal that will not spin usually has a jam. Bones, fruit pits, glass or utensils can wedge the flywheel. We cut power, use a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit to free the flywheel and remove the object. Then we test the motor and press the reset button if needed.

    Leaking Disposal Repair in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    Leaks can come from the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube or the disposal body itself. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty, and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed. In that case, replacement is the better option.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Catalina Foothills

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. We check the reset button, breaker, wall switch and wiring. Air switches and batch-feed lids are tested too. Most electrical issues are simple to fix once found.

    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    A disposal that grinds but drains slowly often has a clogged discharge tube or trap arm. Grease, rice and coffee grounds are the usual culprits. We clear the line with the right tools and check for proper slope. We also explain what not to put down the disposal.

    Noisy or Grinding Disposal Repair in Catalina Foothills

    Loud metal-on-metal sounds may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove debris. If the bearings are failing, the noise will continue. In that case, we can quote a quieter replacement unit.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    Food buildup and grease in the chamber and baffle create odors. Arizona's hard water can add scale that traps food particles. We clean the splash guard and chamber and check the drain for buildup. Replacing a worn splash guard often helps control smell.

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in Catalina Foothills

    A failed disposal can back water into the sink and dishwasher. If water is rising or leaking onto the floor, turn off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us. We respond quickly to stop the backup and repair or replace the unit. That helps prevent damage to cabinets and floors.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    Many jams and minor leaks can be fixed for much less than a new unit. If the disposal is over eight to ten years old, leaks from the body, or has a failing motor, replacement is usually the better value. We provide both prices when it is close. You decide what makes sense for your kitchen.
