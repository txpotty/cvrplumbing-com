    Burst and Leaking Pipe Repair in Catalina Foothills

    Pipes burst from freezing, high water pressure, corrosion or a failed fitting. Arizona hard water causes pinhole leaks in copper that can suddenly widen into a spray. We shut off water, find the damaged section and replace it with copper or PEX. We also check your water pressure, since pressure above 80 PSI stresses the whole system.

    Emergency Slab Leak Response in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    Most Arizona homes are built on concrete slabs, and leaks under the slab can show up as warm spots on the floor, a running meter or the sound of water with everything off. We isolate the hot or cold side so you can keep partial water service. Then we locate the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment. Repair options include direct access through the slab, rerouting the line through walls or attic, or a partial repipe.

    Sewer Backup and Main Line Emergencies in Catalina Foothills

    Sewage coming up through a tub, shower or floor drain is a health hazard and needs fast attention. Roots, grease and broken pipe are the usual causes. We clear the main line from a cleanout with a cable machine or jetter, then camera it to find the cause. If the line is broken, we explain repair options before you leave the house that day.

    Water Heater Emergencies in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    A leaking tank, a failed gas valve or a dripping relief valve can leave you without hot water or with water spreading across the garage floor. Hard water builds sediment that shortens tank life in Arizona. We repair valves, thermocouples, elements and connections on brands like Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White and Rinnai. If the tank has failed, we can usually install a replacement the same day.

    Overflowing Toilets and Fixture Emergencies in Catalina Foothills

    A toilet that overflows repeatedly or a faucet that will not shut off can flood a bathroom fast. We clear the clog, replace failed fill valves, flappers or cartridges and check the supply stop. If the fixture shutoff is seized, which is common with older multi-turn valves, we replace it with a quarter-turn stop. That gives you reliable control next time.

    Gas Line Emergencies in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    A rotten egg odor, hissing near a gas appliance or a dead patch of grass over a buried line can signal a gas leak. Leave the home first and call the utility or 911 from a safe spot. Once it is safe, our licensed plumbers locate the leak, repair or replace the line and pressure test the system. We then relight pilots and check appliance connections.
