    Lasting Sewer Replacement Services in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Catalina Foothills

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It requires only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are completed in one day.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    Some lines have severe sagging, wrong slope or are too shallow for trenchless methods. In these cases, open-trench replacement is the best option. We excavate, remove the old pipe and install new PVC with proper bedding. We restore the trench after inspection. This method allows full correction of slope problems.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Catalina Foothills

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup catches debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and eliminates corrosion. Many Arizona homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are prone to failure. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the extent of damage. Replacement ends ongoing root problems.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Catalina Foothills

    Many Arizona homes are built on slab foundations with drain lines underneath. When these lines fail, replacement requires tunneling or rerouting. We evaluate options to minimize impact on your home. Rerouting through walls or attic space is sometimes possible for branch lines. We explain the cost and disruption of each approach.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Catalina Foothills, AZ

    A cleanout provides easy access for future cleaning and inspection. Many older homes lack an exterior cleanout. We install one during sewer replacement. This saves time and money on future maintenance. It also helps in emergencies.

    Commercial Sewer Replacement in Catalina Foothills

    Commercial buildings often have larger sewer lines and grease issues. We replace commercial laterals and coordinate with businesses to reduce downtime. We may install grease interceptors as needed. We handle permits and city coordination. Our crews work efficiently to restore service.
