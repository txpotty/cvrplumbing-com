    Cracked Toilet Bowl Replacement in Glendale

    Cracks in the bowl usually start near bolt holes or at the base where the porcelain meets the floor. A crack below the waterline will leak, sometimes slowly and sometimes all at once. We drain the toilet, remove the tank and bowl, and install a matching bowl on a new seal. The tank goes back on with new gaskets and bolts.

    Chipped or Stained Bowl Replacement in Glendale, AZ

    Deep chips can harbor bacteria, and hard water can leave rings and stains that no cleaner removes. If the rest of your toilet works well, replacing only the bowl can restore a clean look for less than a full new toilet. We match color, especially on biscuit or bone fixtures, so the tank and bowl look right together. White is easiest to match across years.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Glendale

    Water dripping from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually means a failed gasket or corroded hardware. In some cases the porcelain around the bolt holes has cracked, which calls for a new bowl. We inspect the bolt holes closely and replace gaskets, washers and bolts with stainless or brass hardware. The repair stops the drip and protects the floor below.

    Brand-Specific Bowl Replacement in Glendale, AZ

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all sell bowls and tanks separately for many two-piece models. We use the model number stamped inside the tank or under the lid to find the right match. Some combinations are discontinued, and in those cases we find a compatible option or recommend a full replacement. We order parts quickly from local suppliers.

    Freeze-Cracked Bowl Replacement in Glendale

    In higher-elevation parts of the state, cold snaps can freeze water left in toilets in cabins, casitas or vacant homes. Ice expands and cracks the bowl trap or tank. We replace damaged bowls and show you how to winterize toilets in buildings left unheated. Adding a little RV antifreeze to the bowl and tank can prevent repeat damage.

    Commercial Toilet Bowl Replacement in Glendale, AZ

    Commercial bowls see heavy use and occasional abuse in restaurants, gyms and offices. We replace cracked floor-mount and wall-hung commercial bowls and reconnect flushometer valves.     Emergency Bowl Replacement in Glendale

    A bowl that cracks suddenly can dump water across the floor and into walls or ceilings below. Shut off the angle stop behind the toilet and call us right away. We stop the leak, remove the broken bowl safely and install a replacement or a full new toilet as fast as possible. We also advise on drying and checking for water damage.

    Bowl Replacement Versus Full Toilet Replacement in Glendale, AZ

    A bowl alone can cost $100 to $350 depending on brand, while a complete new toilet starts around $200 for a basic model. If your tank is older or the model is outdated, a full replacement often makes more sense. If your tank is newer and in good shape, a bowl swap preserves your investment. We compare both options before you decide.
