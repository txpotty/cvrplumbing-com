    Long-Lasting Sink Drain Replacement Services in Glendale, AZ

    Pop-Up Drain Replacement in Glendale

    The lift-rod pop-up is the most common bathroom drain and the one that wears out most. We remove the old body, clean the flange seat, and install a new assembly with a fresh pivot ball and gasket. The lift rod is adjusted so the stopper seals and opens fully. This also fixes leaks from the pivot nut on the back of the drain.

    Push-Button and Click-Clack Drain Installation in Glendale, AZ

    Many homeowners switch to push-button drains to get rid of the lift rod entirely. These drains open and close with a press on the stopper. We confirm your sink has an overflow or choose a non-overflow version, then seal and test it. They are easy to clean and work well with modern faucets.

    P-Trap Replacement in Glendale

    Traps fail from cracks, corrosion and repeated clog clearing. We replace old chrome or thin plastic traps with heavy-wall PVC or polished brass that matches exposed plumbing. Each trap is sized correctly and aligned so slip joints seal without strain. A new trap is one of the most cost-effective fixes under a sink.

    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in Glendale, AZ

    When a leak comes from where the drain enters the wall, the trap arm or the stub may be cracked or corroded. Older homes in Arizona sometimes have galvanized drain stubs that rust from the inside. We cut back to sound pipe and transition to new PVC or ABS with approved fittings. This keeps the trap properly vented and draining.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Glendale

    Vessel sinks and many glass basins use grid or umbrella drains without overflow holes. Using the wrong drain can cause slow draining or water spilling through the overflow port. We fit the correct drain type and height for your bowl. We also make sure the tailpiece length matches your vanity.

    Hard-Water Drain Damage Repair in Glendale, AZ

    Arizona's mineral-heavy water builds scale inside pop-up bodies and on stopper rods. Over time this freezes the mechanism or snaps the pivot rod. We remove scaled parts and install new drains with smoother materials that resist buildup. We can also suggest softening options to protect the new drain.

    Commercial Lavatory Drain Replacement in Glendale

    Commercial restrooms use offset grid drains and heavy-duty traps that see constant use. We replace these with ADA-compliant offset tailpieces and can add trap insulation kits where required. Work is scheduled to keep restrooms in service. Multi-sink restrooms can be completed in one visit.

    Drain Replacement vs Drain Repair Costs in Glendale, AZ

    Replacing a single gasket or slip nut costs less up front, but on an older drain the next part often fails within months. A complete drain replacement usually costs only modestly more and resets the clock. We explain both options and the price of each. Most customers choose replacement once they see the condition of the old parts.
