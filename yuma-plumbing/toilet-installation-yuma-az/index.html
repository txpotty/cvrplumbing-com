    Quality Toilet Installation Services in Yuma, AZ

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Yuma

    In new homes and additions, we set toilets after the finished floor is in so the flange sits at the right height. We verify the drain is properly vented and the rough-in matches the fixture you selected. Each toilet gets a new wax or wax-free seal, a stainless supply line and a quarter-turn angle stop. We flush-test and check for leaks before final inspection.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Yuma, AZ

    Remodels often raise the floor with new tile, which can leave the old flange too low. We use flange extenders or replace the flange so the seal compresses properly. If you are moving the toilet, we can relocate the drain, which on a slab foundation requires cutting and patching concrete. We coordinate timing so the toilet goes in after tile and grout have cured.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilet Installation in Yuma

    Comfort-height toilets put the seat at about 17 to 19 inches, which is easier on knees and backs. For accessible bathrooms, we follow ADA guidelines for seat height, centerline distance from the side wall and grab bar clearance. We can install elongated bowls and add blocking for grab bars while the wall is open. These upgrades are popular with homeowners planning to age in place.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Yuma, AZ

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. They free up floor space and make cleaning easy, but they need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep or a furred-out chase. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is best done during a remodel when the wall is open.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Installation in Yuma

    Smart toilets and bidet seats from TOTO Washlet, Kohler and Bio Bidet need a water connection and usually a nearby GFCI outlet. We install the T-valve or dedicated supply, mount the seat and test spray, heat and dryer functions. If an outlet is needed, we coordinate with a licensed electrician. We also explain basic maintenance like cleaning the nozzle and filter.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Yuma, AZ

    Modern WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and dual-flush models offer an even lighter option for liquid waste. In a desert climate where water conservation matters, swapping an old 3.5 gallon toilet makes a real difference on your bill. Some local water providers have offered rebates for high-efficiency toilets, so it is worth checking with yours. We help you choose a model with a strong MaP flush rating.

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Yuma

    We install floor-mount and wall-hung commercial toilets with Sloan or Zurn flushometer valves for offices, restaurants and retail spaces. Commercial installs must meet accessibility and fixture count requirements, and we work with your contractor to pass inspection. We can also install sensor-operated flush valves to reduce maintenance and improve hygiene.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Yuma, AZ

We inspect the fixture for shipping cracks before setting it, since damaged porcelain should go back to the store. We supply the seal, bolts and supply line so you do not have to.
