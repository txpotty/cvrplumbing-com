    Standard Toilet Replacement in Yuma

    A standard swap includes shutting off water, draining and removing the old toilet, scraping the old wax and inspecting the flange. We set the new toilet on a fresh seal with new closet bolts and connect a braided stainless supply line. After leveling and caulking the base, we run several test flushes. The old toilet is hauled away with the packaging.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Yuma, AZ

    Water is precious in the desert, and older toilets are one of the biggest indoor water users in a home. WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and dual-flush models add a lighter flush for liquids. We recommend models with high MaP scores so you save water without double flushing. Ask your water provider whether it currently offers a toilet rebate.

    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Yuma

    Cracks in the tank or bowl can leak slowly or fail all at once. A hairline crack below the waterline is not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes rarely last. We replace the cracked toilet quickly and check the floor for any water damage. If only the tank is cracked and the model is still made, we can sometimes replace the tank alone.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Yuma, AZ

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, which means fewer leak points and easier cleaning. They cost more and weigh more, but many homeowners like the clean look. We install one-piece models from TOTO, Kohler and American Standard. Our team handles the extra weight carefully to protect your floors.

    Comfort-Height Toilet Replacement in Yuma

    Replacing a standard-height toilet with a comfort-height model raises the seat by about 2 to 3 inches. That small change makes a big difference for seniors and anyone with knee or back issues. We often pair this upgrade with an elongated bowl and soft-close seat. The swap uses the same drain location, so no extra plumbing work is needed.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Yuma, AZ

    Landlords and property managers rely on us for durable, low-maintenance toilets in rentals. We can replace several toilets in one visit and standardize on a model with easy-to-find parts. That cuts down on future service calls and tenant complaints. We coordinate access with tenants so units stay usable.

    Commercial Toilet Replacement in Yuma

    For businesses, we replace worn commercial toilets and flushometer valves with modern high-efficiency fixtures. We can switch manual flush valves to sensor-operated models to improve hygiene. Work is scheduled around business hours to minimize disruption. We also check that replacements meet accessibility requirements.
