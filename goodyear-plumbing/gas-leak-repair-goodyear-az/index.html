    Gas Leak Detection in Goodyear

    Some gas leaks are obvious from the rotten egg smell, while others are slow and hard to find. We pressure test the system with a gauge to confirm a leak, then isolate sections to narrow it down. Electronic combustible gas detectors and leak solution pinpoint the exact fitting or pipe. Accurate detection keeps the repair small.

    Underground Gas Line Repair in Goodyear, AZ

    Older coated steel yard lines corrode over time, especially where the coating has been damaged. Lines feeding pool heaters, detached garages and outdoor kitchens are common trouble spots. We call Arizona 811 to mark utilities, then excavate and replace the leaking section with polyethylene pipe and tracer wire. New risers transition safely above ground.

    Appliance Connector Replacement in Goodyear

    Flexible connectors on ranges, dryers and water heaters can crack, kink or loosen. Older uncoated brass connectors are a known hazard and should be replaced. We install new coated stainless steel connectors with proper shutoff valves and sediment traps. Each connection is leak tested before the appliance is relit.

    Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Goodyear, AZ

    Leaks inside walls, attics or garages usually occur at threaded fittings or unions. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and damaged pipe with approved materials. Where access is difficult, rerouting with CSST can avoid extensive wall damage. All work is pressure tested before the gas is turned on.

    Red Tag Gas Repairs in Goodyear

    When the gas utility finds a leak or unsafe condition, it may lock the meter or red tag an appliance. Service cannot be restored until a licensed contractor repairs the problem. We make the repair, perform the required pressure test and provide documentation. We then coordinate with the utility or inspector to restore service.

    Propane Gas Leak Repair in Goodyear, AZ

    Rural homes often run on propane from an above-ground or buried tank. Leaks may occur at regulators, tank connections or underground lines. Because propane is heavier than air, leaks can pool in low areas. We test the full system, repair the leak and confirm correct regulator pressures.
