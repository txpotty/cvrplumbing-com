    Reliable Walls Waterproofing Services in Goodyear, AZ

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Goodyear

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra reinforcement before new tile is set.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Goodyear, AZ

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Goodyear

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We locate the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area surfaces.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Goodyear, AZ

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. In Arizona, irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are common culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Goodyear

    Basements are less common in Arizona, but homes in higher-elevation and hillside areas often have them. Monsoon rain and snowmelt can push water through foundation walls and cracks. We seal cracks with injection products, apply interior or exterior waterproofing and install drainage where needed. A sump pump may be added if groundwater collects.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Goodyear, AZ

    Washing machine hoses, utility sinks and water heaters can all leak into nearby walls. We replace burst-prone rubber hoses with braided stainless hoses and install leak pans and shutoff valves. Walls around water heaters and laundry boxes can be sealed to resist water damage. These small upgrades prevent costly drywall repairs.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Goodyear

    Restaurants, gyms, salons and offices often have restrooms, locker rooms and kitchens with constant water exposure. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with durable membranes. Work can be scheduled after hours to limit downtime. We also repair the plumbing behind those walls when leaks are found.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Goodyear, AZ

    Waterproofing a shower or wall costs far less than repeated drywall, cabinet and flooring repairs. Ignoring moisture also invites mold, which can be expensive to remediate. A typical shower wall waterproofing project falls in the low thousands, while exterior block sealing varies with wall size. We give you a written quote so you can compare options clearly.
