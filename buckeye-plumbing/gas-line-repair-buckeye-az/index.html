    Gas Leak Detection in Buckeye

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead plants over a buried pipe or a gas meter that moves with everything off are classic leak signs. We isolate sections of the system and use electronic combustible gas detectors to narrow the source. Soap solution confirms leaks at fittings, valves and unions. Once we find the leak, we give you a written repair price before starting.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Buckeye, AZ

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or to a pool heater. Over time the wrap fails and the pipe corrodes, especially where sprinklers keep the soil wet. We call Arizona 811 to mark utilities before digging, then replace the line with polyethylene pipe and tracer wire or coated steel where needed. Trenching is backfilled and the yard is cleaned up when we finish.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Buckeye

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, can crack and leak. We replace them with new coated stainless connectors sized for the appliance and install accessible shutoff valves. Ranges, dryers, water heaters and furnaces all need proper connections and sediment traps where required. These quick fixes eliminate a common leak source.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Buckeye, AZ

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is common in newer Arizona homes, especially in attics. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow jacket CSST needs proper bonding. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding to the electrical system. This lowers the risk of a lightning-related leak during monsoon storms.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Buckeye

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and approved thread sealant. Rusted pipe in garages or exterior walls is replaced before it becomes a hazard. We pressure test the system after every repair.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and BBQ Lines in Buckeye, AZ

    Outdoor living is a big part of Arizona homes, and many backyards have gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills. These lines face sun, heat and irrigation water that shorten their life. We repair or replace outdoor gas lines and check that they are sized for the appliance load. Proper sizing keeps pool heaters from starving other appliances.
