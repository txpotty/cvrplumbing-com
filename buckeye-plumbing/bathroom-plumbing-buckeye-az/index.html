    Complete Bathroom Plumbing Services in Buckeye, AZ

    Toilet Repair in Buckeye

    A running or leaking toilet can waste hundreds of gallons a day. We replace worn flappers, fill valves and flush valves, and we fix loose or rocking toilets by repairing the flange and installing a new wax ring. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down rubber parts, so flapper replacement is common here. We also repair cracked tank bolts and leaking supply lines.

    Toilet Installation and Replacement in Buckeye, AZ

    When a toilet is cracked, inefficient or simply outdated, replacement is often the best choice. We install high-efficiency WaterSense toilets, comfort height models and wall-hung units from brands like TOTO, Kohler and American Standard. Every installation includes a new wax ring, closet bolts and supply line. We haul away the old toilet.

    Faucet Repair and Replacement in Buckeye

    Dripping faucets and low flow are often caused by worn cartridges or mineral-clogged aerators. We replace cartridges and seals in Moen, Delta, Kohler and other brands. When a faucet is corroded or the finish is worn, we install a new one and replace the supply lines and angle stops. Hard water scale is the most common reason faucets fail early here.

    Shower Repair and Valve Replacement in Buckeye, AZ

    Showers that drip, fluctuate in temperature or will not shut off usually need a new cartridge or valve. We replace pressure-balance and thermostatic cartridges and full shower valves inside the wall when needed. When the wall must be opened, we access from the back side whenever possible. We also replace showerheads, diverters and tub spouts.

    Shower Installation in Buckeye

    We install new shower valves, rain heads, handheld sprayers and body sprays for remodels and upgrades. Proper valve depth, blocking and waterproofing integration are critical, so we coordinate with your tile contractor. We also install drains for curbless and walk-in showers. Every system is pressure tested before walls are closed.

    Sink Repair and Installation in Buckeye, AZ

    We repair leaking sink drains, broken pop-up assemblies and failed supply lines. For new vanities and sinks, we install undermount, vessel and pedestal sinks with matching faucets and drains. We replace corroded sink drain assemblies and P-traps to stop leaks and odors. Clean, secure connections protect your cabinetry.

    Bathtub Faucet and Drain Replacement in Buckeye

    Tub faucets and spouts wear out from hard water and daily use. We replace tub spouts, diverters and valve trim and can upgrade older two-handle valves to single-handle pressure-balance units. We also replace tub drains and overflow assemblies to fix leaks below the tub. These repairs help prevent water damage to ceilings below.

    Commercial Bathroom Plumbing and Urinal Installation in Buckeye, AZ

    Businesses need durable restroom fixtures that handle heavy use. We install and repair commercial toilets, flushometers, urinals and ADA-compliant sinks and faucets. We work with Sloan, Zurn and other commercial brands. Work can be scheduled after hours to avoid disrupting customers and employees.
