    Whole-Home Electric Tankless Installation in Scottsdale

    Whole-home electric tankless units can supply multiple fixtures at once when properly sized. Models like the Stiebel Eltron Tempra and Rheem RTEX often require multiple dedicated 240 volt circuits. We size the unit based on your peak flow and temperature rise. Warm incoming water for much of the year helps these units keep up. We install isolation valves for easy descaling.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless Installation in Scottsdale, AZ

    Point-of-use units heat water for a single fixture such as a bathroom sink or kitchen faucet. They reduce wait time for hot water in rooms far from the main heater. We install compact models under sinks or in cabinets. These units require less amperage than whole-home models. They are a smart solution for casitas and additions.

    Electrical Panel Review and Upgrades in Scottsdale

    Electric tankless water heaters can require a large amount of amperage depending on size. We review your panel capacity before installation. If your panel cannot support the unit, we coordinate with a licensed electrician for an upgrade. Proper wiring prevents tripped breakers and overheating. Planning ahead avoids costly surprises.

    Converting from Tank to Electric Tankless in Scottsdale, AZ

    Switching from a tank water heater to electric tankless can free up space and reduce standby energy loss. We remove the old tank and install the new wall-mounted unit. Water lines are rerouted and new isolation valves are installed. We also confirm electrical capacity and coordinate new circuits. The result is a cleaner, more efficient setup.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Scottsdale

    Hard water can cause scale buildup in tankless heat exchangers and heating elements. Scale reduces efficiency and can trigger error codes. We recommend a water softener or scale inhibitor to protect your unit. We also install service valves for periodic descaling. Regular maintenance helps extend the life of your tankless heater.

    Electric Tankless for Casitas and Additions in Scottsdale, AZ

    Casitas, guest suites and home additions often sit far from the main water heater. Running long hot water lines wastes water and energy. An electric tankless unit installed near the fixtures provides fast hot water. We size the unit for the space and its fixtures. This is an efficient solution for detached living spaces.

    Electric Tankless Replacement and Upgrades in Scottsdale

    Older electric tankless units may struggle to keep up or show frequent error codes. We replace aging units with newer, more efficient models. Upgrades may include higher kilowatt ratings or better flow control. We check wiring and plumbing connections during replacement. A new unit can restore reliable performance.

    Electric Tankless Installation Cost in Scottsdale, AZ

    Point-of-use electric tankless units generally cost much less than whole-home systems. Whole-home installs may require multiple circuits and sometimes a panel upgrade, which adds cost. We provide a written quote that includes the unit, plumbing and coordination with electrical work. We also compare operating costs with tank water heaters. That helps you decide if tankless is the right fit.
