    Kitchen, Bath and Laundry Drain Clearing in Scottsdale

    Slow sinks, gurgling tubs and overflowing laundry standpipes usually come from grease, hair, soap scum and mineral scale. Arizona hard water leaves calcium deposits that narrow pipes and catch debris faster than in soft-water areas. We clear lines with the right size cable for each fixture and avoid harsh chemicals that damage pipes. If clogs keep coming back, we camera the line to find the reason.

    Main Line and Sewer Drainage in Scottsdale, AZ

    When several fixtures back up at once, the problem is usually in the main line to the city sewer or septic. Tree roots, especially from mesquite, palo verde and ficus, find their way into older clay and cast iron laterals. We clear the main through a cleanout and inspect it with a camera so you can see breaks, bellies or root intrusion. Repair options include spot repairs, lining or replacement.

    French Drain Installation in Scottsdale

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects water and carries it away from problem areas. It works well along foundations, behind retaining walls and in low spots that hold water after storms. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so fine desert soil does not clog the system. The drain is sloped to an outlet, dry well or approved discharge point.

    Channel and Trench Drains in Scottsdale, AZ

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send monsoon runoff straight into a garage or under a door. A channel drain with a grate catches that sheet flow before it reaches the building. We use durable polymer or concrete channels from brands like NDS and set them flush with the surface. The channel ties into solid pipe that carries the water to a safe outlet.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Solutions in Scottsdale

    Gutters and scuppers concentrate a lot of roof water into a few points, often right next to the foundation. We connect downspouts to buried solid pipe and run it to pop-up emitters or a dry well well away from the house. This keeps expansive clay soils from swelling and stressing your slab. It also stops erosion that undermines walkways and landscaping.

    Dry Well Installation in Scottsdale, AZ

    Where there is no place to discharge water on the surface, a dry well lets it soak into deeper soil. Caliche layers can block absorption, so we test the soil and dig through hardpan when needed. Many Arizona jurisdictions have rules on dry well registration and placement, and we follow them. A properly sized dry well with a sediment chamber can handle repeated monsoon storms.

    Commercial Drainage Services in Scottsdale

    Restaurants, retail centers and office buildings need floor drains, grease lines and parking lot drainage that keep working under heavy use. We jet grease-laden kitchen lines, clear floor drains and repair catch basins. Scheduled maintenance plans reduce emergency backups that close a business for the day. We can work after hours to avoid disrupting customers.
