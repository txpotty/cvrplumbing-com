    Lasting Grease Trap Repair Services in Scottsdale, AZ

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Scottsdale

    Baffles slow incoming water so grease can rise and solids can settle. When baffles crack, corrode or break loose, grease passes straight through the trap. We remove damaged baffles and install new ones made for your trap model. In concrete interceptors, we can repair or rebuild baffle walls. Restoring baffles brings back proper separation and helps you stay compliant.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Scottsdale, AZ

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Scottsdale

    Leaks can come from cracked trap bodies, failed gaskets or loose pipe connections. Indoor leaks can damage floors and create slip hazards in the kitchen. We locate the source and replace gaskets, seal fittings or repair cracks when possible. Outdoor leaks can contaminate soil and lead to odors around the building. If the body is badly damaged, we recommend replacement.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Scottsdale, AZ

    Damaged lids and failed gaskets let odors escape and create safety hazards. Outdoor risers can crack from vehicle traffic or ground movement. We replace lids, install new gaskets and repair or replace risers. Traffic-rated lids are installed where trucks or cars drive over the interceptor. These repairs keep odors in and keep people safe.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Scottsdale

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is essential for a trap to pass inspection.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Scottsdale, AZ

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup. A camera inspection helps us confirm the line is clear and check for damage. If we find broken or sagging pipe, we can repair the section. This keeps water and waste moving freely.

    Emergency Grease Trap Repair in Scottsdale

    A failed trap during a busy shift can force you to stop service. We respond quickly to grease trap emergencies and work to get you running the same day when possible. Our crews carry common replacement parts and pumping equipment. We can pump, repair and test the trap in one visit in many cases. Fast service limits lost revenue and health code concerns.

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Scottsdale, AZ

    Simple repairs like gasket or tee replacements usually cost far less than a new trap. Major damage such as a cracked tank body, failing concrete or a badly corroded steel unit may make replacement the better value. We compare repair cost, remaining life and compliance risk for you. Older steel traps often rust through and are good candidates for upgrade. We give you options and pricing in writing so you can decide.
