    Main Water Line Repair in Scottsdale

    Your main water line runs from the meter box to the house and is under constant pressure. When it leaks, you may notice a spinning meter dial with all fixtures off, low pressure or a wet spot that never dries. We locate the leak, expose the pipe and splice in a new section with approved fittings. If the line is old galvanized or polybutylene, we will recommend a full replacement before more breaks occur.

    Underground Sewer Line Repair in Scottsdale, AZ

    Buried sewer laterals crack from root intrusion, soil settling and age. We camera the line to find the exact spot, then excavate and replace the damaged section with PVC. Shielded couplings make a watertight connection to the existing pipe. We confirm proper slope and flow before backfilling and compacting the trench.

    Irrigation and Yard Line Repair in Scottsdale

    Drip lines and sprinkler supply pipes are often shallow and easy to break during landscaping or by roots. A broken irrigation main can run up a water bill fast in summer. We repair PVC and poly irrigation mains, replace failed backflow connections and fix the supply line to the valve manifold. We can also reroute vulnerable lines away from trees.

    Pool and Casita Supply Line Repair in Scottsdale, AZ

    Many properties have buried water lines feeding a pool auto-fill, a guest casita, an outdoor kitchen or a detached garage. These lines are often longer than the main service and harder to locate. We use tracer wire and acoustic equipment to find leaks along the full run. Repairs are made with direct-bury rated pipe and tested before we close up.

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Scottsdale

    Galvanized steel pipe rusts from the inside, and polybutylene pipe becomes brittle and splits without warning. If your underground service is either material, repairs are usually temporary. We replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often by pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. A new line restores pressure and removes the risk of repeated leaks.

    Emergency Underground Pipe Repair in Scottsdale, AZ

    A ruptured main line can flood a yard, undermine a driveway or leave you with no water at all. We help you shut off the meter valve over the phone, then dispatch a crew to locate and repair the break. We restore service as quickly as possible and keep you informed throughout. If the street side of the meter is leaking, we will help you contact the water utility.

    Commercial Underground Pipe Repair in Scottsdale

    Commercial buildings, HOA common areas and multifamily properties often have larger and longer buried water and sewer lines. Leaks under parking lots or sidewalks need careful locating to avoid unnecessary concrete cutting. We work with property managers to schedule repairs that limit disruption to tenants. We also document the repair with photos and test results.

    Underground Pipe Repair Cost Comparison in Scottsdale, AZ

    A single point repair on a shallow water line is the most economical option. Pipe bursting or pulling a new line through the old path costs more but avoids open trenching and long-term risk. Full trench replacement is sometimes needed when the old route is blocked or the pipe is too damaged. We explain each method and its cost so you can choose the best value.

    Common signs include a water bill that jumps without explanation, a meter that moves with all water off, low pressure, and wet or sinking spots in the yard. A hissing sound near the meter box can also point to a leak.
