    Custom Shower Installation Services in Scottsdale, AZ

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Scottsdale

    Walk-in showers are a popular upgrade, with large tiled spaces and frameless glass. We install the valve, showerhead, optional handheld and drain, and set the pan or mortar bed slope. A properly sloped floor keeps water moving toward the drain. We pressure-test and flood-test before tile goes on.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Scottsdale, AZ

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most requested bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height. On slab foundations, the drain may need to be moved, which involves cutting concrete. The result is a safer, more accessible shower.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Scottsdale

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and give a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproofing.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Scottsdale, AZ

    Fiberglass and acrylic shower units are affordable and quick to install. We set the base, connect the drain and install the valve and trim. Prefab units are ideal for guest baths, rentals and casitas. We make sure the base is fully supported so it does not flex or crack.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Scottsdale

    Rain showerheads, body sprays and dual heads need careful planning for valve capacity and water pressure. We install thermostatic valves with volume controls and diverters to run multiple outlets. We also confirm your water heater can keep up with higher flow. The result is a spa-like shower that works as intended.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Scottsdale, AZ

    Accessible showers need low or no thresholds, handheld showers on slide bars and properly placed controls. We install grab bar blocking while walls are open and set valves within easy reach. These features help seniors and people with mobility needs bathe safely. We follow ADA guidelines when requested.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Scottsdale

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and sloped ceilings.

    New Construction Shower Installation in Scottsdale, AZ

    For new homes and additions, we rough in shower valves, supply lines and drains during framing. Valves are set to depth for the planned wall finish, and drains are located for the chosen pan. We pass rough-in inspections and return to install trim after tile. Our scheduling keeps your build on track.
